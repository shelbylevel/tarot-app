# The Robin Wood Tarot: A Narrative Guidebook

## How to Use This Guidebook

These entries are written to be read like short stories about each card, not looked up like definitions. Each one begins with a few keywords, tells you what is happening in the picture and what that might stir in you, and ends with a question to sit with.

They are written for upright cards only, because the Robin Wood deck was designed to be read without reversals. Every card carries its full range of light and shadow in one direction.

The entries interpret the imagery instead of describing it. You can see the picture for yourself, so the text tells you what to draw from it. Robin Wood's own advice was that the meaning in your gut, with the cards actually spread in front of you, beats any book, this one included.

**Reversed meanings.** Robin Wood designed this deck to be read without reversals, so every upright entry already holds the card's full range. If you choose to read reversals anyway, each card has an optional reversed entry beneath it. They follow her own logic: usually the card's energy is blocked, turned inward or running to excess; for the hardest cards (the Tower, the Ten of Swords, the Five of Pentacles) a reversal more often means release or recovery; and for the brightest cards it means dimmed or delayed, not turned to misery. Many of her cards already hold an either/or, such as a gift arriving or leaving, or a haven that is also a trap, and a reversal often picks up the other half.

## The Major Arcana

The Majors are the big weather of a life: the forces that move through you more than they wait for your permission. When several appear together, you are in a chapter, not a moment.

### 0 · The Fool

*Beginnings, trust, dancing at the edge*

Your enthusiasm is running on knowledge you hold only in your head; it hasn't yet sunk into your gut. That gap is the whole story of this card. You feel free, brave and untroubled partly because you haven't met the consequences yet, and your eyes are on the far goal instead of the ground under you.

Don't squash the joy; it's what gets you started. But take the quieter voices seriously, a friend's hesitation or your own instinct, which may be warning you while you're too happy to listen. Bring a little discipline along and this beginning can be wonderful instead of merely reckless. Underneath it all is innocence, a pure-hearted hunger for experience that hasn't learned restraint yet.

**Sit with:** What am I too delighted to notice right now?

#### Reversed · *Two ways to miss the moment*

Both versions come from the same fear. Either the cautionary voice has been drowned out and you're leaping on pure momentum, or it has grown so loud that you never take the first step. The knowledge hasn't reached your gut, so what looks like courage or caution is really fear in disguise, and carelessness, vanity or plain indecision can take the wheel.

**Sit with (reversed):** Am I rushing to outrun the fear of starting, or stalling to avoid the fear of falling?

### 1 · The Magician

*Skill, focus, everything on the table*

You are further along than you think. Skill, resources and timing are lining up, and what's needed is a decision to use everything together rather than one tool at a time. This card has the confidence of someone ready, and something is about to give you the chance to show it.

Your power here comes from balance: acting in the visible world while staying tuned to what you can't see, and bringing thought, will, feeling and groundedness into the same moment. Quiet confidence beats showing off. Begin, and let ease be part of the evidence that you're in the right place. Originality, imagination and a diplomat's touch help you bring others along.

**Sit with:** What do I already have that I keep treating as if it were missing?

#### Reversed · *Skill scattered or sealed*

The tools are all there, but you're either using them one at a time, showing off instead of making something, or not trusting them at all. The balance between what you can see and what you can't has slipped, so one side is running the show and the quiet confidence has gone shaky. Insecurity or clumsy handling can lead to a misjudgment that costs something.

**Sit with (reversed):** Am I performing my skill, hiding it, or actually using it?

### 2 · The High Priestess

*Intuition, stillness, two kinds of knowing*

Trust your inner knowing and practice patience. There is practical wisdom within reach, but the deeper answer comes through stillness, dreams and the sense you can't quite explain. Some of what you want to understand isn't fully visible yet, and that's by design.

Don't force the reveal. You are early in a season of growth, even if you already know a lot, and the hidden thing is close to coming clear. Hold the reasoned and the intuitive ways of knowing together and let them correct each other. Quiet leadership, not noise, is what's being asked of you. Good judgment is available to you; the future simply stays clouded for now. She may be you, or a woman who matters to you.

**Sit with:** What do I know that I haven't given myself permission to know?

#### Reversed · *Intuition muffled or hoarded*

The inner knowing is there but drowned out by noise, or you hold it privately when it was meant to be shared. Reasoned knowledge and intuition have stopped correcting each other, and the reveal is being delayed or forced at the wrong moment. Strong feeling or conceit about what you already know can crowd out real intuition.

**Sit with (reversed):** What am I keeping to myself that wants to be shared, or ignoring that wants to be heard?

### 3 · The Empress

*Abundance, nurture, a life that grows*

Fertility in every sense: a creative project, a relationship, finances, a home or body being nurtured, or an actual pregnancy. Everything here is ripening because it is being cared for over time. The card carries security, warmth and good counsel, and says you can afford to be generous.

You don't need to chase anything. Tend what you have, rest in the abundance, and let things grow at the pace of living things. It can mean a competent, loving person in your life, or the part of you that calmly builds a future for the people you love. Good advice, safety, and a good deal of quiet work behind the scenes are part of it.

**Sit with:** What in my life is ready to be fed rather than fixed?

#### Reversed · *Nurture turned inward or withheld*

The creative richness is blocked, or you're pouring so much into other people that nothing is left for the one doing the tending. It can also be abundance that smothers. Growth stalls when it isn't fed, and that includes you. Dissatisfaction, an involved matter coming unraveled, or a barren stretch can follow.

**Sit with (reversed):** Who is taking care of the person who takes care of everyone?

### 4 · The Emperor

*Structure, authority, the cost of control*

Order, authority and protectiveness: a person, or an inner voice, who lays down structure and can be counted on to hold the line. Maturity has replaced impulse and decisions come from calm reasoning instead of heat. This is stability, and it is real.

The shadow is rigidity. Someone who prefers control may mistake it for care, and may not want to hear what others think. Use this energy to set clear boundaries, finish what you start and lead steadily, and check whether the structure still serves the people living inside it. It can be a father figure, or anyone whose authority rests on reason and conviction.

**Sit with:** Where am I protecting something by controlling it, and where is it asking for room?

#### Reversed · *Structure gone rigid or hollow*

Authority has hardened into control, or the backbone has gone missing and the boundaries no longer hold. The stability that used to protect is now confining, or it has quietly crumbled while everyone kept following the rules. At its extreme it turns into self-importance, a ruler who mistakes obedience for respect.

**Sit with (reversed):** Is my structure holding me up, or holding me in?

### 5 · The Hierophant

*Convention, ritual, form without life*

This card is about conformity, and it asks how much of your life is shaped by what's expected. Where ritual has gone hollow and form has outlived purpose, you may feel flat, constrained or quietly resentful. It can also point to someone who needs you to stay in line. Form is being put ahead of function, and old ways are held on to long after they stopped serving, often out of timidity.

Not every tradition is a trap, but ask which ones still nourish you. You may be trading your own thinking for the comfort of belonging, or you may be the one controlling others. Real guidance trusts you to think. If you feel boxed in, you're allowed to question the box.

**Sit with:** What am I doing simply because it's expected?

#### Reversed · *The rules begin to loosen*

This is the questioning that conformity was designed to prevent: a first glimpse of the box from outside it. It can also run the other way, with conformity gone quiet and internal, a rule you obey in your head that nobody is actually enforcing. It can also show up as hollow rebellion: eccentricity for its own sake, or a generous gesture that is really about appearances.

**Sit with (reversed):** Whose rule am I still obeying?

### 6 · The Lovers

*Union, balance, being fully seen*

Real union takes two whole people. It's balance between giving and receiving, leading and following, seeing and being seen, in a bond where both become more themselves. It can be romantic, or any joining of opposite qualities, including the ones inside you.

The invitation is to be unguarded: honest, trusting, free to be what you are without fear. Shared choices matter, and so does shared work, from making a home to providing for it. Something fruitful grows when two sides of a thing choose to move forward together. It can describe a couple who got here by working through trials together.

**Sit with:** Where could I trade rigidity for trust?

#### Reversed · *Balance tipped*

One person leads and the other always follows, or both stay guarded, so union exists without trust. It can also be the imbalance within yourself, where an opposite quality you're meant to hold has been shut away and the partnership feels lonely. Silence, where thoughts go unshared, is often the first sign, with unreliability close behind.

**Sit with (reversed):** Where have I stopped meeting someone halfway, or meeting myself?

### 7 · The Chariot

*Harmony in motion, steering without force*

You're moving ahead without strain because you've found a way to make two opposite pulls work together instead of against each other: ambition and rest, head and heart, the practical and the dreamy. That is harmony in motion, and it carries you faster than force would.

The control here is gentle, closer to music than muscle. If you tried to win by force it would go badly. Truth is your only protection, and something larger than you is guiding the direction. Trust the balance, and remember that even perfect balance keeps changing. Troubles fall behind you, and there may be work, travel and a real sense of purpose.

**Sit with:** What two things in me are asking to pull the same cart?

#### Reversed · *The two pulls stop cooperating*

One of your drives is overriding the other, so the harmony breaks and momentum stalls. You may be steering with force where music was needed, or letting go so completely that you've lost direction. Quarrels and a sense of hopes coming undone follow when neither pull is heard.

**Sit with (reversed):** Which of my pulls am I overriding?

### 8 · Strength

*Gentleness, courage, a wild thing befriended*

True strength is love that isn't afraid. The powerful, instinctive part of you, with its appetite, temper and drive, responds to patience and trust better than to force. Courage here is quiet, and it transforms what it touches because it isn't trying to dominate.

You may be able to soothe other people's pain too, and help them find their own way through. The lesson is self-control born of connection, not suppression. What you meet with compassion learns to calm itself, so show your roughest feelings the gentleness you'd show a frightened friend.

**Sit with:** What would happen if I met my own intensity with tenderness?

#### Reversed · *Courage turned inward, or gentleness turned to force*

Your fierce side is being handled with pressure instead of trust, whether by clamping down on it or white-knuckling through it. The courage is real but unseen, and the self-doubt around it is doing more harm than the wildness ever would. Left unchecked, the force can turn domineering, and a fear of being alone often sits underneath.

**Sit with (reversed):** Am I trying to tame my intensity or to silence it?

### 9 · The Hermit

*Solitude, wisdom, carrying a light*

You are being called inward, and it is purposeful. After a long journey you've gathered what matters and shed the rest, and you now have enough light to find your way and to guide others. This is a time of reflection, study and withdrawal to find your center.

Don't mistake solitude for failure. You are the experienced version of the beginner you once were, and what you carry is lighter and more powerful now. You may be the one who guides someone else, or you may be about to meet someone who can guide you. Prudence and good counsel come with the quiet.

**Sit with:** What would I hear if I gave myself an evening of real quiet?

#### Reversed · *Solitude gone too long, or too shallow*

Withdrawal has become avoidance, or you hold a light you won't share, or you can't get quiet enough to find any. The inward journey needs both stillness and a willingness to eventually come back down. A hasty retreat, or caution with no plan behind it, is the usual flavor.

**Sit with (reversed):** Is my solitude feeding me or hiding me?

### 10 · Wheel of Fortune

*Change, cycles, the emotional roller coaster*

Everything is in motion and your feelings are moving with it, from elation to despair and back. This card describes the ride more than the destination. When things matter too much, every small event swings you to an extreme, which is the signal to look for steadier ground.

Change follows a pattern, and the pattern repeats. Where you are now is a position on the cycle, not the whole of it. If you can hold that loosely, accept what comes and learn from it, you can ride without getting dizzy. Don't invest too heavily in wherever the ball lands today. It also promises that troubles are in sight of ending, and that luck, for better or worse, is on the move.

**Sit with:** What am I treating as permanent that is really just a position on the wheel?

#### Reversed · *The swing settling, or the grip tightening*

You're thrown harder because you're gripping the ride, or the extremes are flattening into numbness. Often it signals release: the pattern is slowing down and the emotional swing is finally easing. It can also mean luck turning and an unexpected interruption throwing things off.

**Sit with (reversed):** Am I fighting the swing, or letting it settle?

### 11 · Justice

*Fairness, clear sight, consequences*

A matter is being weighed, and it will be weighed fairly. This card values clear, impartial thinking, unswayed by fear or passion, that looks at the whole situation instead of applying a single rule. It may involve legal or formal matters, or simply the moment you take an honest look at your own conduct.

Look at every side, including the uncomfortable ones, and be willing to see what each party needs. Decisions made slowly and calmly tend to be truer. Expect balance and an honest accounting, and trust that clarity will give you a courage that anger cannot. Negotiations and agreements fall under this card too, along with equality and rightness.

**Sit with:** If I looked at this situation as an impartial stranger would, what would I conclude?

#### Reversed · *The scales unbalanced*

Somewhere a side is not being weighed: a bias, a skewed accounting, a judgement avoided because it would cost too much. Clear sight has gone blurry, and the fairness you want to give or receive isn't getting through. Prejudice, intolerance or a punishment out of proportion may be in the room, including a legal matter that goes against you.

**Sit with (reversed):** Where am I not weighing both sides?

### 12 · The Hanged Man

*Surrender, a new view, chosen stillness*

You have chosen to stop and look at the situation from a completely different angle. It's a deliberate surrender, and the discomfort is part of the price of what you're about to learn. Nobody is imposing this; it's a willing sacrifice made for the sake of insight, especially the inner or spiritual kind.

Suspense is part of it. Don't try to leave early or fight the stillness. Your old priorities are changing, and the dullness of the familiar view is being cleaned out by a new one. Answers will come from willingness more than effort, so stay long enough to be changed. Your life has been interrupted, and the interruption is the teacher.

**Sit with:** What would shift if I stopped pushing and let myself see this upside down?

#### Reversed · *A pause that doesn't teach*

You're hanging in the pose, but your mind hasn't turned over. The suspension is stalling instead of surrendering, or the sacrifice has outlived its lesson. The other possibility is that you're finally ready to come down. It can also be a gesture or sacrifice that achieves nothing because the effort isn't really there.

**Sit with (reversed):** Am I waiting for insight, or avoiding a decision?

### 13 · Death

*Endings, release, a door that does not reopen*

A complete and irreversible change has arrived, and the old path is closed. It's the end of an era, a profound discovery, a shift of direction. It is not physical death. The fear comes mostly from not being able to see what's next, which is natural.

Courage is available precisely because you have no choice. What's arriving carries freedom and rebirth, even sweetness, though the road is rough. Let the old thing end and walk toward the new with a clean heart. Resisting will only make it harder. It is also a reminder of mortality, and of a discovery that redirects your life.

**Sit with:** What am I holding on to that has already ended?

#### Reversed · *Resisting what has already ended*

The old path is closed, yet you keep walking back to it. The change may also be happening slowly and privately, so it doesn't feel like a clean break. Either way, the passage doesn't work until you let it be over. Heaviness, inertia or a low mood may come with it, because the change is ponderous and you're dragging your feet.

**Sit with (reversed):** What's already over that I keep reopening?

### 14 · Temperance

*Balance, skill, grace in juggling*

Grace comes from balance among many things. You are managing your resources and appetites with skill, and you're at home in both the practical and the intuitive worlds. Everything in moderation, including moderation, with an ease that lets you move freely through any situation.

It may mean you need to stretch your resources carefully for a while, or that you already are and doing it well. The path isn't easy at first but it gets smoother, and a period of difficulty is nearing its end. Temperance here is vitality, not deprivation. Patience and thrifty management buy real security, and even moderation shouldn't be taken to an extreme.

**Sit with:** Where have I gone to an extreme, and what would a middle path feel like?

#### Reversed · *One ball takes over*

The juggling falls apart because one thing is getting all your attention at the cost of the others, or you're swinging between extremes instead of finding the middle. Healing may also be rushed before the work is done. With other people it shows up as competing interests, too much caution, or someone who is hard to work with.

**Sit with (reversed):** What am I giving all my attention to at the expense of everything else?

### 15 · The Devil

*Greed, grasping, the trap you can walk out of*

This card is greed, and it asks where appetite has become your master. A desire, maybe for money, comfort, approval or control, is so strong that you won't let go of any of it, and so you're stuck. You're trapped only because you refuse to give up the part you can't carry out.

What you want is good in itself; the trouble starts when wanting rules you. Greed isolates, shrinking your world until there's room only for you and the thing you crave. This is a clear warning: loosen your grip, give up the whole to keep what matters, and the way out is already open. It can also be bondage to an ideal, bad influence or advice, or a fateful choice that your future hangs on.

**Sit with:** What would I have to release to be free?

#### Reversed · *The fist begins to open*

You've seen the trap and started to loosen your grip, or the grasping has gone underground and wears a respectable face. Release is possible, but greed turned inward as self-denial is still a grip. A rest, and a new direction, are close if you keep opening it.

**Sit with (reversed):** Am I opening my hand, or hiding my grasping?

### 16 · The Tower

*Collapse, revelation, ground cleared for new building*

Something built on a false premise is coming down, suddenly and loudly. Status, security, a friendship or an ambition that was never on firm ground is being cleared away. It hurts, and it's also a mercy, because it couldn't have held. Disgrace or humiliation may come with it.

Ask yourself which assumption it was resting on. If you can find it, you may soften the blow. If the collapse has already happened, the worst is over and you're free to build more honestly. Sometimes the structure was really a monument to ego, and its loss makes room for growth.

**Sit with:** Which of my certainties has never been tested?

#### Reversed · *The collapse you're postponing*

The false premise has been spotted but not yet surrendered. You're shoring up what you already suspect won't hold, or living through a slow, quiet unraveling instead of one crash. The longer the propping, the higher the eventual cost. Left alone, the old arrangement keeps pressing on you, and money may be lost holding it up.

**Sit with (reversed):** What am I propping up that I already know won't last?

### 17 · The Star

*Hope, renewal, quiet inspiration*

The storm has passed and something quiet and clear is restoring you. This card carries hope, inspiration and an easy flow between your inner and outer worlds, a feeling of being guided and replenished. It's the gift of meditation, dreams and a gentle opening to something larger.

Rest in it. Let what flows in from deep within refresh the everyday, and let your senses and spirit nourish each other. New thoughts may arrive unannounced, as inspiration does. You're asked to stay open, reflective and unhurried, and to trust the old, fresh knowing this moment offers. It is a blend of the best of the past and present, with an awareness of two worlds and bright prospects ahead.

**Sit with:** What helps me feel, even briefly, that I am connected to something larger?

#### Reversed · *Hope dimmed or turned inward*

The replenishing is slow, or you won't let yourself be refilled. Hope hasn't gone, it's gone quiet, or you're dreaming without drawing anything from the water. Healing is still happening, just more privately than you hoped. Indifference, delay or a quiet loss of faith can creep in if you stop tending it.

**Sit with (reversed):** What would I need to feel safe enough to hope?

### 18 · The Moon

*Wildness, instinct, the uncivilized self*

Something wild in you is waking, and the civilized parts of your life may feel suddenly confining or unfamiliar. It is restlessness, an urge to go feral, a pull between what's tamed and what's instinctive. Hidden things are stirring, and not all of them are tidy.

Stay on the difficult path between the two. Acknowledge the wildness without being swallowed by it, because you can only help others who are lost if you stay reachable yourself. Notice whether this energy is freeing you or making you predatory, and whether it's your own or someone else's. The traditional warning still applies: stay on your path, because something unseen, a deception or a hidden opponent, may be near, and companions may be out of their element in the dark.

**Sit with:** What part of me has been quietly asking to howl?

#### Reversed · *Wildness locked away, leaking out sideways*

What you won't let out on purpose escapes in small, sideways ways: a white lie, a bit of sleight of hand, a minor slip. In the other direction, the restlessness settles into stillness and the mist lifts, and something unexpected comes to you without a price attached. Either way, the wild thing is quieter than it was.

**Sit with (reversed):** Where is my wildness leaking out because I won't let it out deliberately?

### 19 · The Sun

*Joy, vitality, plain happiness*

This is plain, uncomplicated joy: success, contentment and the end of trouble. It carries the freedom of someone who doesn't have to explain or apologize for being happy, and a security so deep you can simply play. It may mean a good marriage, or a child with a sunny nature.

You've come a long way from the beginning, through solitude and back to simple delight. You don't need to concentrate on living; you just live. The best may even be on its way, so let yourself be cheerful, radiant and entirely seen. Accomplishment and material comfort come with it.

**Sit with:** What would I do today if I trusted that it was safe to enjoy it?

#### Reversed · *Joy that doesn't feel safe yet*

The happiness is there but dimmed, held back because the security that lets you play feels shaky. Don't force brightness. Find out what makes you doubt it's safe to be openly happy. Lesser joys, delayed security or a gap between you and loved ones may be the price in the meantime.

**Sit with (reversed):** What would make it safe enough to be happy where people can see?

### 20 · Judgement

*Rebirth, awakening, answering the call*

Nothing is condemned here; you are being reborn. After a period of fire and purification you're wiser, stronger and more loving, and something new is emerging from what burned away. It's a spiritual awakening, a change of consciousness, a renaissance.

No one fails in this version; there are only lessons learned. Leave the old self behind without regret and let love be your answer to all of it. When it's time to go back out into the world, you'll do it as someone who has been remade. There may be an accounting first: atoning for something, or settling what you owe, before the new purpose can take hold.

**Sit with:** What old version of myself am I ready to leave in the ashes?

#### Reversed · *A call refused, or a verdict you made up*

The rebirth is being delayed because you won't answer, or you're stuck in the fire punishing yourself for a lesson you've already learned. This card never condemns, so any judgement you feel is yours. Avoiding an obligation or dodging a confrontation often sits behind it.

**Sit with (reversed):** Am I punishing myself for a lesson that's already learned?

### 21 · The World

*Completion, wholeness, a door opening*

Completion, and with it, possibility. You've integrated what you've learned and are at ease with it, joyful and unhindered. Every ending carries a beginning, and from here anything is open. The only limits are the ones you set yourself.

What you create now can take many forms, and not all births are babies. You're at home in both the visible and invisible worlds, in every direction. Celebrate what you've finished, then notice that a new cycle is quietly opening. A change of place, the admiration of friends and a wide field of possibilities may come with it.

**Sit with:** What have I finished that deserves to be acknowledged?

#### Reversed · *A finish without integration*

Completion is delayed or hasn't sunk in. You've reached the end but haven't made peace with the journey, so the door to the next cycle stays half-open. Something is unresolved, and it asks for acknowledgment before you move on. The advance can feel discouragingly small, and stagnation sets in if you won't finish what you started.

**Sit with (reversed):** What am I carrying across the finish line that I haven't made peace with?

## Wands

*Fire · will, creativity, drive · noon in the desert*

The suit of Wands is energy: desire, ambition, passion and the courage to act. It is fire, so it grows, warms, inspires and burns, and its lessons are about focusing will, using energy wisely and not being consumed by your own flame.

### Ace of Wands

*A spark, a seed of life, creative force*

A burst of creative life is starting. It's the seed of every possibility in this suit: energy, vitality, inspiration, even new life. Rest and action, mind and dreams, are held in balance within it, so this power can be used without burning you out.

You don't need a plan yet, only to notice that something has lit you up and to follow it. This is virility, fertility, adventure, an idea beginning to evolve. Greet the spark with joy and let it show you what it wants to become. An inheritance, a birth or a stretch of adventure can be part of it.

**Sit with:** What is making me feel alive right now?

#### Reversed · *A spark with no air*

The creative impulse is real but smothered, mistimed, or scattered across too many fires at once. Something wants to begin, and it needs space and oxygen before it needs a plan. Left alone, it becomes a false start or a stagnant idea.

**Sit with (reversed):** What is waiting for me to give it room to burn?

### Two of Wands

*Waiting well, vision, the long view*

You've done the planning and now you're waiting on results. It's an in-between time that can feel like nothing is happening, yet it's rich with inner growth. You have the world in view and the preparations behind you, so patience becomes a form of courage.

Waiting actively, learning, reflecting and enjoying the present, is very different from waiting passively. Don't fill the pause with anxious planning. Notice where you are, deepen what you know, and stay ready for what's coming. Kindness, generosity and creative intelligence come along with the pause.

**Sit with:** How can I use this pause instead of just enduring it?

#### Reversed · *Waiting turned to hiding*

The fear of what's out there keeps you behind the wall, or impatience pulls you out before the preparation is finished. Either way, the active waiting has stopped being active. Good beginnings can sour, and others may begin to dominate the plan.

**Sit with (reversed):** Am I waiting well, or hiding?

### Three of Wands

*Ships coming in, trust in the future*

What you invested in is returning. You have a clear sense of what the future needs, and the courage you showed in taking risks is being rewarded. The past taught you, the present is open, and the future is bright.

Good business, solid foundations and success in a venture are all in view. Stay alert and keep a firm grip on what's coming, because you know what you're doing. You've earned the right to feel confident about the road ahead.

**Sit with:** What did I send out a while ago that I should be expecting back?

#### Reversed · *Returns delayed or off course*

What you sent out is slow to come back, has been blown off course, or you're watching the wrong horizon. You may have overextended, or planned for the arrival and not for the voyage. A venture may sour, and your grasp of what's ahead is shakier than you think.

**Sit with (reversed):** Did I plan for the journey, or only the arrival?

### Four of Wands

*Celebration, harvest home, a joyful pause*

Harvest home. You've worked, and now there is shelter, celebration and rest, a moment of harmony that may take the form of a wedding, a milestone or a safe haven. Opposites, like work and rest or two partners, are cooperating.

Rejoice together and honor the labor that made this possible. Remember that some work remains and that after the party you'll go back to it. This is a gentle, lifted-up time, so enjoy it fully without worrying about what's next. New prosperity and a settled home life are part of the blessing.

**Sit with:** What have I earned that I haven't let myself celebrate?

#### Reversed · *Celebration kept private, or performed*

The joy is quiet, thin, or put on for show. The shelter feels shaky, or the party is smaller than you hoped. Real contentment may still be here, but more inward than public. Ingratitude or dissatisfaction with what you have can spoil it.

**Sit with (reversed):** Am I celebrating, or performing a celebration?

### Five of Wands

*Friction, competition, a storm of energy*

There's a lot of energy and no clear direction, which is why this reads as conflict. Competing ideas, people or impulses are colliding, and in the dust you may have lost sight of what's actually happening. It can also be an inner struggle.

A pattern is almost there, the pieces nearly in order, but until they come together the effort is struggle for its own sake. Ask whether this is chaos about to organize itself or order about to fall apart, and step back far enough to see. Unsatisfied desires and indecision usually feed the friction.

**Sit with:** Am I struggling against something, or only struggling?

#### Reversed · *The fight turned inward*

The struggle moves inside, where your own ideas and impulses collide, or you avoid a confrontation that would clear the air. The pattern may also finally be settling into order. Trickery or a tangle of involvements can complicate it.

**Sit with (reversed):** Whose fight is this really?

### Six of Wands

*Victory, recognition, the crowd cheering*

A public win. You've been recognized, and there is applause, good news and a reward for hard work. The victory is of the mind and spirit, not force, and it comes with joy and a sense of rightness.

### Queen of Swords

*Sharp intelligence, honesty, clear boundaries*

Sharp, perceptive and honest: a person who has known loss and grown strong because of it. She approaches things indirectly, combining intuition with intellect. Her words can cut.

At her worst she's controlling; at her best she's clear-eyed and fair. She's unconventional and not easily pushed. Be direct with your own truth, mind where your tongue is sharpest, and mind her buttons, too.

**Sit with:** What truth about this situation am I willing to say plainly?

#### Reversed · *Clarity as a wall, or honesty withheld*

Her sharpness has hardened into coldness that keeps people at a distance, or the truth she knows stays unspoken. The gift of seeing clearly is meant to connect, so ask whether your honesty serves the truth or serves your distance. Keenness sharpened to cruelty, gossip and quarrelsomeness are the shadow forms.

**Sit with (reversed):** Is my honesty bringing me closer to people, or keeping them away?

### King of Swords

*Authority, intellect, judgement without sentiment*

Authority and the cool exercise of judgement: a commander who treats the world as a series of problems to solve. He's decisive, rigorous, firm in friendship and enmity, and he wants to control the situation.

He may help you overcome your problems or see you as one. His shadow is rigidity, suspicion and being closed to new ideas. Borrow his clarity, and notice where thinking has hardened into dominance.

**Sit with:** Where could I use clearer, firmer thinking, and where am I being too hard?

#### Reversed · *Judgement without fairness*

Authority turns cold, cruel or manipulative, or steps back and abandons the decision. Control over the truth is not the same as clarity. Pig-headedness, craftiness and untrustworthiness can show up.

**Sit with (reversed):** Am I judging fairly, or just firmly?

## Pentacles

*Earth · body, work, home, resources · twilight in a garden*

The suit of Pentacles is the material world: money, work, health, home and the patience that growth requires. It is earth, so it is slow, quiet and sensual, and its lessons are about delayed gratification, rewards after the job is done, and gaining wisdom through solitude.

### Ace of Pentacles

*A seed of prosperity, a gift from the earth*

A real, tangible opportunity arrives: prosperity, comfort, a good start to something that can be built. It carries material and spiritual riches, luxury and meaning both, and the knowledge that things should be balanced, with spirit above appetite.

Gardens like this are made, so it also asks for work. It's a path you've kept well, leading to a place that is yours to fill. Decide what you want to put there.

**Sit with:** What would I like to grow from this, and what will I need to plant?

#### Reversed · *An opportunity left unplanted*

The seed isn't being planted, or is taken for granted until it wilts. The empty place waiting for your choice stays empty. Unhappiness with what you have, or a misuse of power, may be at the root.

**Sit with (reversed):** What am I leaving unplanted?

### Two of Pentacles

*Juggling, adaptability, a light touch with a lot to carry*

You juggle competing demands with ease and a light touch. It's balance in the middle of change, a sense of play, and the confidence of someone who has chosen this role. You make it look effortless.

Even when you're out of your element, you can stay in control. Falling is only a danger if you grab at things. Whistle while you work, and let your imagination fly.

**Sit with:** How can I keep this balance with a little more laughter?

#### Reversed · *A dropped ball*

The juggling collapses, a ball falls, or play turns to panic. The ease was a skill, and it needs attention. Forced cheerfulness can cover the panic.

**Sit with (reversed):** Which ball am I pretending I'm not dropping?

### Three of Pentacles

*Craft, collaboration, mastery recognized*

Your skill is appreciated and your care is rewarded. This is mastery earned through years of practice, which now frees you to enjoy your work. Recognition, achievement and perhaps promotion are on the way.

Craft done for love tends to be paid well. A natural next step is to teach what you know, though even mastery is accomplished one step at a time. Take pride in the work and let it be seen. Rank or power may follow.

**Sit with:** What work of mine am I proud of that deserves to be shown?

#### Reversed · *Work that isn't seen*

Your skill goes unrecognized, collaboration breaks down, or pride keeps you working alone. Mastery without feedback can plateau. Sloppiness, mediocrity, or caring more about the payoff than the craft can undermine it.

**Sit with (reversed):** Who could help me see my own work?

### Four of Pentacles

*Holding tight, security, a closed fist*

You're holding on too tightly. Fear of loss has made your world small and cold, and security has become isolation. You absorb everything and give nothing back.

The festival is happening just beyond your walls, and you won't turn around. Saving wisely is different from clutching. Hold what is yours firmly enough to keep it and loosely enough to live, and ask who might see you if you opened the door.

**Sit with:** What am I guarding so tightly that I've stopped living with it?

#### Reversed · *The grip loosening, or tightening*

Often release: you begin opening your hand and the cold lets up. Or the fear of loss tightens and the walls grow thicker. Gains may pause, and opposition or a reversal of fortunes can show up.

**Sit with (reversed):** What would I gain by opening my hand a little?

### Five of Pentacles

*Hardship, being left out, a door you haven't tried*

A season of hardship: loneliness, lost money, poor health, feeling left out in the cold. Help is much closer than it looks, and what stands between you and it is probably pride or fear. Notice who is next to you in the cold.

The warmth may be an institution, a person or a door you've never tried. Ask yourself why you haven't knocked. Sharing the burden with someone suffering alongside you often brings a comfort neither of you can find alone. Poor health, a spiritual drought, or lovers who can't find a place to meet fit here too.

**Sit with:** Where could I ask for help, and what has stopped me?

#### Reversed · *Help finally accepted, or refused*

The door opens and warmth reaches you, the recovery beginning. Or pride keeps you out in the cold a little longer. Help is still within reach. Lessons in charity, fresh courage and perhaps temporary new work can follow.

**Sit with (reversed):** Who might I let in?

### Six of Pentacles

*Generosity, giving and receiving, fair exchange*

Help changes hands, and it's thoughtful: a gift, a repaid favor, financial aid, or the pleasure of giving. It's fair, chosen with care, and personal rather than institutional. It can be healing to give or to receive.

Notice whether you're the giver or the receiver, and whether there's enough to go around. Generosity and gratitude both have grace. The lesson of the earlier hardship has been learned, because people are helping each other.

**Sit with:** What am I able to give, and what am I willing to receive?

#### Reversed · *Giving with strings*

The exchange is uneven, the scales are tilted, or generosity creates dependence instead of freedom. Charity that expects something back is a trade. Jealousy, avarice or a bad debt can cause harm.

**Sit with (reversed):** Is my giving free, or does it expect a return?

### Seven of Pentacles

*Patience, tending the crop, a pause before the harvest*

The work is done and now you wait. Growth has been achieved through effort, and you're watching it ripen. This is a time for patience, which is hard to master, when the best action is to do nothing and let the fruit set.

Don't pick early. Resist the urge to dig up the roots to check. Rest, enjoy this moment and trust the labor you've already put in, which will pay off when it's ripe. Surprisingly good news or useful help may arrive.

**Sit with:** What am I trying to hurry that needs more time?

#### Reversed · *Impatience at the harvest*

You dig up the roots to check, or wait so long you doubt the crop was ever worth it. Either way you're judging before the fruit is ready. Worry about money or a poor investment may be part of the picture.

**Sit with (reversed):** Have I waited long enough to judge this?

### Eight of Pentacles

*Apprenticeship, practice, quiet diligence*

Practice, apprenticeship and diligence. You're learning a craft one careful repetition at a time, absorbed and getting better. It can also mean hard work at modest pay.

Mastery comes from repetition. Don't forget to let in some air now and then, because the work leads somewhere and dreaming is part of making. Keep your tools sharp, and keep going.

**Sit with:** What skill am I willing to be a beginner at?

#### Reversed · *Repetition without growth*

Perfectionism stalls the work, the drudgery goes nowhere, or the shutters are closed to the world. Practice should lead somewhere. Vanity, a flat emotional tone, or borrowing money you shouldn't can be part of it.

**Sit with (reversed):** Am I improving, or just repeating?

### Nine of Pentacles

*Self-sufficiency, refinement, a garden of your own*

Self-sufficiency and refinement: a beautiful, secure, self-made life. You have control over your passions and appetites, and you enjoy comfort without greed. There is richness and solitude, and a love of nature.

Ask whether the wall around you is a haven or a cage, whether you chose it freely, and whether the gate could be opened if you wanted. Sometimes safety is bought at too high a price. Enjoy your independence with open eyes. Discretion, quiet achievement and a love of nature belong to it.

**Sit with:** Is my solitude a choice I'm glad to have made?

#### Reversed · *The garden becomes a cage*

The self-sufficiency has become isolation, the gate won't open, or the security feels shaky. Luxury bought with loneliness is expensive. A threat to security may be close.

**Sit with (reversed):** Do I want to stay inside, or can't I leave?

### Ten of Pentacles

*Legacy, family, lasting abundance*

Lasting prosperity: home, family, generational wealth and love, a foundation that will outlive you. All is abundant, spiritually, emotionally and materially. It may be a family or a chosen community.

You could be any of the people in this card. See yourself as the giver, as the child learning to give, or as the generation in between. Think about what you're building for those who come after, and enjoy it now. Positive changes at home are part of it.

**Sit with:** What am I building that I'd be happy to pass on?

#### Reversed · *A foundation less solid than it looks*

The picture looks secure, but strain or instability sits underneath. Family or money matters may need honest attention before they hold. A bad risk, or the loss of belongings or of someone close, may be behind the strain.

**Sit with (reversed):** What's underneath the picture?

### Page of Pentacles

*Study, a new skill, careful dreams*

A careful, studious spirit, methodical, deliberate and a bit old-fashioned. She's learning something in depth and may be bringing news. There's patience in her and a delight in study.

She is grounded and nurturing, with a wildness underneath. Study something slowly and thoroughly, and let your curiosity put down roots. A young person may make you proud.

**Sit with:** What would I enjoy studying slowly and thoroughly?

#### Reversed · *Study stalls*

Distraction, delay or too much theory keeps the learning from taking root. The wildness underneath wants out, but nothing has been started. Wastefulness or muddled thinking may creep in.

**Sit with (reversed):** What would I do if I stopped waiting to feel ready?

### Knight of Pentacles

*Reliability, steady effort, the long haul*

Dependable, hardworking and thorough: a person or quality you can rely on absolutely. He won't be flashy, but he shows up. The cultivation of something has begun, and it will take care.

He's slow and steady and sometimes stubborn, closed to new ideas. It's a good time to commit to the long haul. It can also mean travel.

**Sit with:** Where would steady, unglamorous effort serve me best?

#### Reversed · *Reliability hardened into stagnation*

Steadiness becomes stubbornness, routine becomes a rut, or the effort dries up and nothing moves. Slow and steady needs to include some change. Work troubles, carelessness or deceit may be nearby, and travel is better postponed.

**Sit with (reversed):** Is my reliability still serving me?

### Queen of Pentacles

*Nurture, practicality, a home that welcomes*

Warm, practical and generous: a nurturer who makes things grow, including people. She's caring and hardworking, and she thinks things through quietly. She wants what's good for you, which may be what you need rather than what you want.

She has a sensible love of luxury and a talent for matching people up. Bring her care to the daily, physical side of life. A home she builds is a refuge. Gifts of money, intelligence and thoughtfulness may come your way.

**Sit with:** How can I bring more care to the daily, physical parts of my life?

#### Reversed · *Care that smothers, or neglects the self*

Nurturing tips into hovering, or worry overshadows the practical, or the caretaker is forgotten. The home needs tending too. False prosperity, distrust, and a changeable streak born of fear of failing can show up.

**Sit with (reversed):** Who is tending the tender?

### King of Pentacles

*Abundance, comfort, wealth that has been well tended*

Wealth and comfort that have been patiently built: a generous, reliable, earthy and sensual leader. He enjoys luxury without showing off and has a quiet connection to the earth. He is ruled by planning and the delay of gratification.

He's a father figure you can depend on, but also stubborn and set in his ways, and not fond of adventure. Be as steady as he is, and savor what you have built.

**Sit with:** What does a comfortable, well-built life look like for me?

#### Reversed · *Comfort turned to hoarding*

Wealth is hoarded, comfort becomes indulgence, or stubbornness blocks growth. Or the security begins to slip, which is a reason to look at what you've built. A temper that turns dangerous when crossed, or talents put to petty use, may come with it.

**Sit with (reversed):** Is my comfort feeding me, or fencing me in?
