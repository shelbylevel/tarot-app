# The Wild Unknown Tarot — Complete Guidebook (Upright & Reversed)

## How to Read These

These entries follow the Wild Unknown guidebook's format: a card name, a few keywords, and a short narrative that leans on the imagery. Kim Krans leaves reversals out on purpose, so treat these as an optional layer for when you want one. They never replace the upright card.

Three shapes recur, and which one fits depends on the card:

- **Blocked, internalized, or excessive** is the default. The card's energy is stuck inside you, turned against you, or running too hot.
- **Release** applies to the difficult cards. The grip loosens, the storm thins, or the ending is already behind you.
- **Dimmed or delayed** applies to the bright cards. The gift is real but hasn't arrived, or you can't feel it yet.

Each card has an Upright and a Reversed entry, and each closes with a **Sit with** question, a single prompt to carry with you after the reading.

The courts keep Krans's names (Daughter, Son, Mother, Father). Reversed, they show the shadow side of a person or a part of yourself, not a different person.

## Wands

Ruled by the element of fire, the Wands represent inspiration and creativity. They relate to our ambitions, goals, and dreams. They mark the beginning of all ventures within the mind and spirit. Many Wands in a reading indicates the situation resides mostly in the mind... not yet in the external world.

### Ace of Wands

#### Upright

EXPANSION, INSPIRATION

An extremely high energy card, the Ace of Wands indicates new growth is all around you. Your ideas and outlook will expand, and creativity seems endless. This fertile time can also have literal meanings... Pregnancy or birth may be on the horizon. Though it’s thrilling to embark on your new journey, your good judgement is needed more than ever. Don't rush into it.

**Sit with:** Where is something new already growing in you that you haven't given room to yet?

#### Reversed

UNREALIZED INSPIRATION, CREATIVE BLOCK

The wand still sprouts, but its blossoms stay tight, and the white-hot field around it never spreads into the orange and yellow rays that should stream outward. Those radiating lines are the card's movement, and reversed they go slack. Remember that this may be the same branch the Fool stood on: the opportunity is real, and you haven't stepped onto it. Something is smothering the fire, whether exhaustion, comparison, or waiting for the perfect plan. Give the spark air, and don't ask it to be a bonfire yet.

**Sit with:** What is smothering the spark, and what is the smallest breath of air you could give it?

### Two of Wands

#### Upright

DETERMINATION, DIRECTION

With the Two of Wands, it’s clear you're on your way to success. You've focused on a goal and have boldly pointed all efforts in that direction. Since the suit of Wands deals heavily with the mind, take this time to become aware of your thought patterns, specifically toward yourself. What is the quality and tone of your thoughts? Mostly positive or negative? How well do you treat yourself on the journey to your highest dreams?

**Sit with:** How do you speak to yourself on the way to the thing you want most?

#### Reversed

HESITATION, UNCLAIMED POWER

The two wands angle toward the horizon, close enough to take hold of, and the rainbow bands behind them run flat, energy that is strong but not yet pointed anywhere. Reversed, you keep looking at the wands without reaching, or you grab someone else's and call it your direction. The Two is the card of recognizing your own power, so its reversal is the flinch. Ask what would change if you believed the wands were already yours. Then close your hand around one.

**Sit with:** What would you reach for today if you believed the wand was already yours?

### Three of Wands

#### Upright

ENVISIONING THE FUTURE

The Three of Wands indicates you've had continued support from others (perhaps from your parents or two good friends). With their help, you've formed a sense of self, of your values and morals. But now it's time to rely on yourself for guidance. Clarify your goals, and cast others’ needs and opinions aside. The future is infinite, and it is yours. No one else can see through its layers of light and shadow.

**Sit with:** Whose voice are you still using to describe your own future?

#### Reversed

UNCLEAR VISION, BORROWED DIRECTION

The rainbow swirl at the center of the three bound wands has gone dull, or you keep surveying it from a distance and never step through. The wands' rugged, unpruned branches ask you to trust a rough, wild future, and reversed you want a neat one. You may be following someone else's map because your own feels uncertain. Or you've planned so thoroughly that no wonder is left. Let one branch stay untrimmed and walk toward the swirl.

**Sit with:** Which part of your future are you trying to trim neat before you've walked toward it?

### Four of Wands

#### Upright

COMPLETION, CELEBRATION

You've done it! The Four of Wands is a card of completion. Your labors have been steady and strong, and the harvest will be plentiful. In other words, it’s time to party. There might be an upcoming event to mark this occasion... a graduation, wedding, or celebration of some kind. So enjoy yourself and those you love. This is an exciting and prosperous time.

**Sit with:** Who do you most want beside you when you mark what you've finished?

#### Reversed

DELAYED CELEBRATION, UNACKNOWLEDGED PROGRESS

The crossed wands that look like arms thrown up in joy hang still, and the small diamond where they meet has lost its calm blue center. You've reached the milestone and feel nothing, or you've already moved on to the next mountain. The border of black and white lines, the closed portal that has begun to open, stays shut. Ask what keeps this achievement from landing. Often it's permission to feel it. Pause and let yourself celebrate before you move on.

**Sit with:** What would you have to feel to let this milestone land before you start the next climb?

### Five of Wands

#### Upright

SCATTERED, LACK OF FOCUS, LOST

The Five of Wands is a card of conflict. You may find yourself scattered, distracted, tending to everything and nothing. Since Wands deal with our dreams and goals, it usually means this conflict resides within. Your energy moves outward in many directions, yet you are suffering inside. Take this card as a warning: find ways to bring calm and focus to your mind, or these troubles will only increase.

**Sit with:** Where is your energy flying in five directions, and what is it trying to protect at the center?

#### Reversed

EASING CONFLICT, REGAINING FOCUS

The wands scattered across the monochrome card, each in its own little force field, begin to ease. The lines that cut the card into territories fade, and a conflict loses its charge. You stop spending energy on skirmishes that were never worth it. Or the irritation goes underground instead, swallowed until it sours. Either way, the scatter is settling. Decide what deserves your fire and let the rest burn out.

**Sit with:** Which fight can you let burn out, and which one is truly worth your fire?

### Six of Wands

#### Upright

VICTORY, SUCCESS, RISING UP

From the dark and tangled branches emerges a butterfly. New life takes flight. If you're not reveling in the joy of success already, you will be soon. The Six of Wands is a card of victory, of rising up against the odds. The obstacles have been relentless, but now is not the time to look back upon them. The more pressing question is: Where will you go with your new set of wings?

**Sit with:** Now that you have your wings, where do you actually want to go?

#### Reversed

DELAYED SUCCESS, SEEKING APPROVAL

The green and blue butterfly is clear of the dark heap of wands below her, but she hasn't yet flown. The triumph is real and slow to be seen, by others or by you. The reversed Six also asks whether you're waiting for applause before you let yourself rise, since this card is meant to be an inner victory. Rest on the branch for now. The sky will still be clear when the wings are ready.

**Sit with:** Are you waiting for someone to applaud before you let yourself rise?

### Seven of Wands

#### Upright

COURAGE, INNER STRENGTH

A single wand stands up tall, ablaze with light. The others fall away in shadow. Such is the story of the Seven of Wands. You'll not find any strength or support from others, only yourself. It’s time to be truly courageous and stand up for your beliefs. You may feel overwhelmed with caution and fear, but your internal fire will guide the way.

**Sit with:** What belief would you stand alone for, even with your knees shaking?

#### Reversed

EXHAUSTION, MISPLACED DEFIANCE

The lone wand's white-lit flame either sputters, drained by standing alone, or blazes at everything, including the six wands that only leaned away in surprise. The card is already a short burst of fight-or-flight, and reversed, the burst is spent. Ask whether this stand is truly yours to make. Conserve your fire for what is worth it. A conviction outlasts an outburst.

**Sit with:** Is this stand truly yours to make, or is the fire running on fear and fatigue?

### Eight of Wands

#### Upright

SUDDEN MOVEMENT OR CHANGE

The Eight of Wands strikes like lightning. It's a card of news, change, or clarity in an unresolved situation. You might hear from an old friend out of the blue or receive some surprising news that shifts the direction of your course. This card sometimes means literal movement is on the horizon, so be ready to travel if the opportunity arises.

**Sit with:** What news or nudge have you been half-expecting, and are you ready to move when it comes?

#### Reversed

MISSED MESSAGES, DELAYED NEWS

The rainbow bolt comes down, but you're looking the other way. The eight wands still dance in their circle, and the meaningful coincidence passes unnoticed. Or you act before the alignment arrives, forcing a synchronicity that hasn't happened. Slow down and watch for the small winks. The moment will come again if you're paying attention.

**Sit with:** What small wink have you walked past because you were looking the other way?

### Nine of Wands

#### Upright

STRENGTH, STAMINA, CONFIDENCE

As nines usually imply, your journey is near completion. But on this final stretch you find yourself growing weary; doubt and fear are running through your mind. The Nine of Wands asks you to rally your confidence and realize how far you've come. Lift your eyes and take a few more steps... soon all your hard work will start to pay off.

**Sit with:** How far have you already come that you're too tired to notice?

#### Reversed

GUARDEDNESS, DEPLETION

The log-thick wands, narrowing up toward the crescent moon, either wall you in so completely that nothing can climb them, or the stack has crumbled and left the moon unguarded. The vertical lines that gave the card its steady, protective feel are either rigid or gone. Ask who these walls serve, and when the watch last ended. Let one wand come down and let someone climb the stair, or build the fortress back more gently.

**Sit with:** Who are these walls keeping out, and who might you let climb the stair?

### Ten of Wands

#### Upright

BURDENS, BLOCKAGE, DIFFICULTY

The Ten of Wands is a difficult card to face. Mental or physical burdens have been weighing on your spirit. Over time this leads to hopelessness and depression. You simply can’t "get through" to what you want. You cannot see the way. If this card appears in response to a person or situation, it may be best to simply walk away. But if this card comes up frequently, it indicates you are attracted to negativity. You choose to walk the hard road.

**Sit with:** Which of these burdens did you pick up on purpose, and which could you set down today?

#### Reversed

SETTING DOWN BURDENS, RELIEF

The thick black lines that read as rain begin to let light through, and the wands lying in disarray become visible one by one. This is the first view of what you've actually been carrying. You may finally drop a project or say no, or you recognize that you keep hauling what should have been set down long ago. Either way, put the clutter in order. The way forward is easier to see once the bundle is lighter.

**Sit with:** What have you carried so long you forgot you were allowed to put it down?

### Daughter of Wands

#### Upright

VISIONARY, PASSIONATE

The Daughter of Wands is a free spirit, a truly visionary creature. She usually waits until later to settle down or start a family, as she's busy with her career. She can be stubborn and much stronger than she looks. As with all the wands family, the Daughter makes a dangerous enemy. This card can also represent a woman going through a transformation or spiritual breakthrough.

**Sit with:** What does your wildest vision look like when you let it be as strong as you really are?

#### Reversed

SELF-DOUBT, UNFINISHED CREATIONS

The snake's figure eight around the blossoming wand pulls in, and the artist hesitates. Her red and yellow stand out from the black, but she's convinced she doesn't deserve the room. Or her charm outpaces her follow-through and the wand blooms with nothing finished. If she's you, make one small thing from start to end. If she's someone in your life, give encouragement before judgment.

**Sit with:** What is one small thing you could finish, start to end, to prove you deserve the room?

### Son of Wands

#### Upright

CHARMING, ADVENTUROUS

The Son of Wands is a true charmer. He’s handsome and adventurous and seems to say all the right things. Some call him the "white knight" of the tarot. But at some point you may find he becomes hard to know. Trust your instinct with this one—perfection is an illusion.

**Sit with:** What would you say yes to if it didn't have to be perfect?

#### Reversed

BRAVADO, BURNOUT

The snake's strut and extended tongue still carry swagger, but the heat radiating from his wand has no target. He has one plan and no backup, and when it fails he's already gone. His one-man parade fizzles into smoke. If he's you, build a second plan and be present once the spectacle fades. If he's someone else, notice whether what he offers survives the afterglow.

**Sit with:** What remains when the spectacle is over and no one is watching?

### Mother of Wands

#### Upright

ATTRACTIVE, DOMESTIC, VIBRANT

The Mother of Wands is a vibrant woman and happy mother. Family comes first in her world—she’s very protective of it and is the dominant parent within the home. Though proud and determined, she has enough grace and beauty that you'd hardly notice her forceful nature. Oftentimes she has overcome great pain or trauma in her life. It’s important not to get on her bad side.

**Sit with:** What have you overcome that makes you so fiercely protective of what you love?

#### Reversed

OVER-PROTECTIVE, DEPLETED

The snake curled around her nest of eggs, wand held as armor, tightens until protection becomes possession. Her warm steadiness turns prickly, and she guards the nest so hard that no one can approach. Or she has poured herself so completely into others that her own orange and red lines run to embers. If she's you, ask what you're guarding so fiercely and who needs a way in. If she's someone in your life, set a gentle boundary without matching her heat.

**Sit with:** What are you guarding so tightly that no one can come close, including you?

### Father of Wands

#### Upright

CHARISMATIC, CREATIVE

The Father of Wands is a man of mystique and charisma. Others are drawn to him naturally, and he welcomes them with an open and loving heart. Usually you'll find him involved in the healing arts, as he’s deeply compassionate and in tune with life and nature. He is able to hold steady and still during conflict.

**Sit with:** Where can you hold steady and still while everything around you flares?

#### Reversed

DOMINEERING, DRAMATIC

The red and orange bolt still falls against the black, but he's forgotten what it was for. His flair for the dramatic becomes theatrics, and his command of the room turns into keeping people guessing. Or the master plan has quietly gone stale and he won't admit it. Real authority doesn't need an audience. If he's you, lay the plan on the table and ask who it serves. If he's someone in your life, look past the performance to see if the foundation is still there.

**Sit with:** Does your plan still serve anyone, or are you performing it out of habit?

## Cups

Cups are the suit of emotion. They address the quality of our relationships and our connections to others. They speak of love, friendship, and all that's in between. The suit of Cups is ruled by the element of water. Several Cups cards indicate the situation mostly involves the heart.

### Ace of Cups

#### Upright

LOVE'S BEGINNINGS

Aces mark the beginning of exciting new phases. Since the suit of Cups deals specifically with love and emotion, you're in for a real treat. Expect to enter a blissful time of health, joy, and friendship. A new love could be on the horizon. You may feel that you're making a whole new start... just go with it and allow this feeling to revitalize your spirit.

**Sit with:** What in your heart is ready to begin again, if you let it?

#### Reversed

BOTTLED FEELINGS, EMOTIONAL OVERFLOW

The goblet still glows against its blue, stained-glass field, but the warm orange and yellow that hover above it stay held back, as though contained. The cup holds every emotional state, and reversed, you've sealed it so none can pour. You may be calling everything fine because feeling it all at once seems too much. Or the cup overflows so wildly you can't tell what you're feeling. Name one thing you've been holding in and let it be exactly what it is.

**Sit with:** What have you sealed away that is asking, quietly, to be felt?

### Two of Cups

#### Upright

CONNECTION, LOVE, ROMANCE

Say hello to the much adored Two of Cups. When this card appears, you can anticipate new love to be heading your way. And yes, this usually means romance... but sometimes it points to the birth of a dear and lasting friendship. Either way, the connection between you will be pure, honest, and solid. Open your heart and get ready.

**Sit with:** Where are you ready to be honest and open with someone?

#### Reversed

IMBALANCE, ONE-SIDED LOVE

The crossed red roses, each blooming over the opposite cup, carry the card's meaning: mutual exchange. Reversed, one rose droops or the roses stop crossing. One side gives more than it gets, or you've stopped reaching across to something you love. Red is the only color here, so the life force has been drawn out of the connection. Ask who is carrying the exchange, and whether you can say so with kindness.

**Sit with:** Who is carrying more of this exchange, and can you say so kindly?

### Three of Cups

#### Upright

FRIENDSHIP, JOY, BOUNTY

The Three of Cups is a lovely card. It foresees you in the company of amazing friends, creating memories together that you'll forever hold dear. These are not any ol’ friends; they are people with whom you feel a rare connection. With them you feel truly "yourself." Enjoy each minute of their company. This card can also indicate an upcoming celebration. Love abounds!

**Sit with:** With whom do you feel most like yourself, and when did you last gather?

#### Reversed

ISOLATION, GOSSIP

The three birds' warm red and orange sunset has dimmed, and the branch holds only chatter about who isn't there, or fewer birds than before. Community should leave you warmer. If your circle now fills the silence without feeding you, change the company or your part in it. Or you've drifted from the flock and don't know how to return. The three cups along the bottom are still waiting.

**Sit with:** Is your circle feeding you, or only filling the silence?

### Four of Cups

#### Upright

GREED, DISCONTENT

An outsider would look upon your life and see supportive relationships, pleasures, and even a bit of luxury. But you don't see it that way. Instead, there's discontentment, apathy, and even greed for more. The Four of Cups warns not to take your situation for granted, or the people who supported you along the way. Look around you. What are you truly longing for? Name it.

**Sit with:** What are you longing for that you can't name while the cups sit right in front of you?

#### Reversed

EMERGING FROM APATHY, RENEWED AWARENESS

The rat sprawled over his four cups in the dark has lifted his head toward the white moon spanning the top half of the card. The discontent that kept his eye on the underbelly begins to lift, and he sees what was offered all along. Reversed, you come out of withdrawal slowly and without fanfare. Accept what's within reach before it passes. You don't have to feel eager, only willing to look.

**Sit with:** What has been offered to you all along that you're finally willing to look at?

### Five of Cups

#### Upright

GRIEF, DISAPPOINTMENT

The Five of Cups indicates a time of sorrow. A relationship may fall apart or an old friend could slip away. There will be disappointment and even regret. When this card appears, it’s important not to make any hasty decisions. It may also be helpful to look at the type of expectations you place on others. Perhaps they can never do enough to please you.

**Sit with:** What are you mourning, and what expectations of others are you carrying alongside it?

#### Reversed

HEALING, LOOKING FORWARD

She has stood in the black and white dark long enough to feel all of it, and now she senses the five cups glowing in the lighter space above her. The mane may still be ragged, but the sorrow is no longer the only thing in view. Or you've been pushing the grief away so hard that it can't move through you. Feel it fully, then lift your eyes. Healing begins when you look up, not before.

**Sit with:** Have you let yourself feel this fully, or only pushed it away?

### Six of Cups

#### Upright

MEMORIES, CHILDHOOD, JOY

A card of beautiful implications, the Six of Cups focuses on the magic and innocence of childhood. The roots of years past are alight with color and vibrancy... look back upon them, get lost in your memories. Old friends may surface and seek you out. All reunions will be joyful. Enjoy simple pleasure and wonder in all areas of your life.

**Sit with:** Which memory still has color in it, and what is it asking you to bring forward?

#### Reversed

STUCK IN THE PAST, UNEXAMINED ROOTS

The evergreen above the surface stays calm and stable with its flat black and white lines, while all the color pools in the tangled rainbow roots below. You may live in memory so much that the present feels thin. Or you refuse to look at the roots at all, and they tangle in the dark. Bring the past into the light with kindness. Each root has a story, and each person you meet has theirs.

**Sit with:** What roots are you afraid to look at, and what would a little light show you?

### Seven of Cups

#### Upright

ILLUSION, DECEPTION

The Seven of Cups is not the most welcome card. It indicates you'll face temptation in many aspects of your life. Whether it’s cheating for pleasure or for money, you'll soon realize you've been building a house of cards. You may feel as though you can't see clearly, can't judge right from wrong or up from down. This is the spell of the Seven of Cups. It’s best to remove yourself for a while, step back until you can see straight again.

**Sit with:** Which of these glittering cups would you still want if you could see through the mist?

#### Reversed

CLARITY, MAKING A CHOICE

The seven floating cups steady, the upside-down center cup rights itself, and the moon and sun stop trading places. After the sensory overload, you can tell which cups were smoke and which were real. Choose one. Clarity can feel like loss at first, because dreaming was easier than choosing. The mountain is still there, and it was never as hazy as the mist suggested.

**Sit with:** What did you mistake for smoke that was actually real, and what are you ready to choose?

### Eight of Cups

#### Upright

STAGNATION, ILL HEALTH

The Eight of Cups sends an urgent message to pack your bags and move on. There is no hope of rekindling what's been lost. You must start anew. This card also points to phases of illness and physical stagnation. The message could not be more clear: nothing good remains for you here. Lift your eyes to the horizon, and let your feet lead you forward.

**Sit with:** What have you already outgrown that you keep walking back to?

#### Reversed

CLINGING, RESTLESS DRIFTING

The eight cups left at the foot of the dark mountain have been out in the rain for a long time. You either stay among them, hoping they might mend, or you leave so quickly you can't say what you're walking toward. Moving on is hardest when something good remains. If you've stayed too long, say so. If you left too fast, give the loss its due.

**Sit with:** Did you leave too fast or stay too long, and what do you owe the loss?

### Nine of Cups

#### Upright

BLISS, HARMONY

At last, the card of wishes come true. When the Nine of Cups appears, worries and fears will be cast away. A new phase of peace and harmony awaits. The world seems to be granting your every wish. Good health, happiness, and even material gains are heading your way. Enjoy.

**Sit with:** What does satisfaction feel like in your body right now?

#### Reversed

EMPTY PLEASURE, DISCONTENT

The soft sunset pinks, purples, and blues are still there, and the calm horizontal lines have gone flat. A wish comes true without the joy you expected. You may be indulging to numb instead of enjoy, or chasing more because gratitude never quite settles. The crescent moon reminds you to feel good now, not later. Stop with one small pleasure and see whether you can actually taste it.

**Sit with:** What are you indulging to numb, and what pleasure could you actually taste?

### Ten of Cups

#### Upright

RADIATING ENERGY

The Ten of Cups overflows with positivity. Your goals are being realized, and the excitement surrounding you is magnetic to others. You may feel as though light or color radiates outward from you—almost as if you're shining. Experience sensations like this with an open and joyful heart. Do not doubt this power.

**Sit with:** Where do you feel light moving through you and out toward others?

#### Reversed

DISCONNECTION, DELAYED HARMONY

The ten cups' five beams each, sent across the card and received in turn, no longer circle back. Light goes out and nothing returns, or the other way around. The kinship and harmony of this card are delayed, or the picture of a happy family doesn't match the one you live in. Ask where the sending and receiving lost their balance. Start with one person you can be honest with, even if it's you.

**Sit with:** Where did the sending and the receiving fall out of balance?

### Daughter of Cups

#### Upright

CREATIVE, EMOTIONAL

Creative energy abounds from the Daughter of Cups. She’s romantic, artistic, and easily inspired by her surroundings. A true dreamer by nature, she struggles to stay "grounded" or to deal with any harsh realities that come her way. Conflict of any kind is very disturbing to this gentle creature.

**Sit with:** What does your tenderness want to make today?

#### Reversed

FRAGILE, OVERWHELMED

The young swan's calm water, with its rainbow reflection, ripples and breaks the image apart. She may be overwhelmed by feelings she can't name, or hide behind a rose-colored view that no longer holds. Her innocence can curdle into disappointment fast. If she's you, protect your tenderness without burying it. If she's someone in your life, be gentle and keep your promises.

**Sit with:** What feeling are you rose-coloring because it's too big to name?

### Son of Cups

#### Upright

ARTISTIC, INTROSPECTIVE

Like all of the Cups family, the Son truly excels within the arts. He’s usually a musician or a visual artist of some kind, and he finds success within his field. His natural tendency to look inward adds to his charm and mystique. To others he may seem secretive and even peaceful, while deep inside he carries a dark kernel of intensity.

**Sit with:** What are you carrying quietly that wants a form, a song, or a page?

#### Reversed

MOODINESS, SELF-PITY

The swan gazes to the right with slanted lines behind him, and the rainbow that streamed from his cup is a puddle. His introspection turns self-indulgent, his poetry sappy, his sensitivity hypersensitive. He sulks in the beauty of his hurt instead of tending the wound. If he's you, ask what you're really feeling beneath the mood, then say it plainly. If he's someone in your life, don't mistake dramatic sorrow for depth.

**Sit with:** Beneath this mood, what are you really feeling?

### Mother of Cups

#### Upright

INSIGHTFUL, PSYCHIC

The Mother of Cups rivals the High Priestess with her natural psychic abilities. She's a gentle, tranquil woman whose insights bring healing to those around her. She thrives when amidst her family, the arts, and music or beauty of any kind. Like a true swan, when the Mother of Cups is pushed, she becomes aggressive and defensive. She'll commonly see herself as the victim when distressed in a situation.

**Sit with:** What is your intuition telling you that you haven't yet acknowledged?

#### Reversed

EMOTIONALLY OVERWHELMED, GUARDED

The white swan, facing left against her black sky, curls her wing so tightly over the goblet that nothing can reach it. She has absorbed everyone's feelings and has nowhere to set her own. Or the surface has gone cool and still, because being open has cost her before. The few pale stars dim. If she's you, ask what you're carrying that was never yours. If she's someone in your life, be tender and honest.

**Sit with:** Whose feelings are you carrying that were never yours?

### Father of Cups

#### Upright

DIPLOMATIC, OPEN-MINDED

The Father of Cups has a truly unique and dynamic personality. He's the most feminine of all the fathers within the tarot and usually a patron of the arts. He's a dignified man who supports his family and community. The only thing holding this man back are his deeply rooted insecurities; they are vast and affect his personality in many unpredictable ways.

**Sit with:** Where could you be kind and honest in the same breath?

#### Reversed

AVOIDANCE, EMOTIONAL DISTANCE

The black swan, facing right against the white and rain-streaked ground, holds poised, but his plain black cup may be empty. His diplomacy becomes avoidance, and his calm becomes distance. He smooths every conflict and never says what he needs. He may be keeping the peace to stay in control or to stay hidden. If he's you, give your own feelings the care you give everyone else's. If he's someone in your life, listen for what he is leaving unsaid.

**Sit with:** What have you been smoothing over that you need to say out loud?

## Swords

The Swords are the suit of action and intellect, ruled by the element of air. These dynamic cards address change, conflict, and power. They point to constructive and destructive tendencies within us all. A reading dominated by Swords indicates a situation of turmoil and distress.

### Ace of Swords

#### Upright

TRUTH, MENTAL CLARITY

When lightning strikes, the whole world seems to light up for just one second. It's as though you can "see everything," and this vision lingers on even through the storm. Such is the power of the Ace of Swords. Your mind will become clear, your thoughts precise. It's a great time to make those decisions you've been putting off.

**Sit with:** What do you see clearly right now that you couldn't see yesterday?

#### Reversed

CLOUDED THINKING, HARSH WORDS

The white sword still stands in the center, but the lightning has already flickered out and the storm closes back over it. The clear thought slips past before you can grasp it, or you grasp it so hard that you use the truth as a weapon. The rainbow band wound around the blade in a figure eight is the full, holistic picture, and reversed, you see only one color of it. Wait for the next flash, and when you speak, ask whether you're being honest or only sharp.

**Sit with:** Are you being honest, or only sharp?

### Two of Swords

#### Upright

BLOCKED VISION, STALEMATE

The Two of Swords depicts two opposing forces that have reached a stalemate. You cannot progress forward with either a relationship or a life decision. There's an answer that's being blocked—maybe by choice? Many times this card will indicate you do not wish to face the truth the light will reveal.

**Sit with:** What are you not letting the light show you?

#### Reversed

TRUTH REVEALED, DECISION FORCED

The moon that covered the sun at the crossed blades' peak moves off, and the light you've refused to see comes through. The stalemate breaks, often because you can no longer hold the swords crossed. It may feel like relief or like being caught out. Either way, you can finally decide, because you can finally see. Don't spend the clarity on blame. Spend it on the choice you've been putting off.

**Sit with:** Now that you can see, what decision have you been putting off?

### Three of Swords

#### Upright

BETRAYAL, HEARTBREAK, TURMOIL

A dark and complex card, the Three of Swords is rarely a welcome sight. Its wrath may come in the form of lies, betrayal, cheating, or heartbreak. There will be emotional entanglement and confusion. Do not try to make any decisions while in this state. Wait for your heart and spirit to mend.

**Sit with:** What are you willing to let mend before you decide anything?

#### Reversed

HEALING, LETTING GO OF PAIN

The blood-red threads wrapped around the three entangled blades begin to slacken and drip less. The wound closes, or you're finally ready to stop pressing on it. The light that always showed past the dark background gets closer. Or you've replayed the pain so often that it has become part of how you hold yourself. Let the story soften. You aren't forgetting what happened. You're letting it stop bleeding through everything else.

**Sit with:** Which part of this pain are you ready to stop pressing on?

### Four of Swords

#### Upright

STILLNESS, MENTAL POWER

Though threatening swords loom above, the lamb sits in stillness without fear. The Four of Swords says it’s time to look inward and find the mental power to cope with the pressures. It's important to rest. Seek meditation or find literature that focuses on ways to quiet the mind. Take this time to recuperate and move inward before those swords strike.

**Sit with:** What would real rest look like for you this week?

#### Reversed

RESTLESSNESS, AVOIDING REST

The lamb's third-eye sphere flickers, and the four evenly spaced swords overhead feel closer than their even spacing suggests. Stillness isn't giving you refuge. You may be dragging yourself back into action before you've recovered. Or hiding in rest because the world above looks threatening. Ask which it is. Real rest restores your mental power, and avoidance only postpones what's waiting beyond the clear sky.

**Sit with:** Are you resting to recover, or hiding from what waits above?

### Five of Swords

#### Upright

SELF-DESTRUCTION

Yet another card of warning, the Five of Swords depicts the destruction of the self. You'll be lured into another situation or argument you know to avoid. Your selfishness and desire for power will lead you to defeat. Pain and discord will result.

**Sit with:** What fight do you already know to walk away from?

#### Reversed

FORGIVENESS, ENDING THE FIGHT

The divided worm lies pierced by its five swords, and someone finally withdraws them. You stop fighting a fight that was costing more than you could win, or you stop cutting yourself down. This is the quiet end of spite and of the self-criticism behind it. Or the cruelty simply goes underground and turns inward, where no one sees it. Look for where the hostility went, and put the sword down there too.

**Sit with:** Where did the hostility go when you stopped fighting out loud?

### Six of Swords

#### Upright

HOPE AT LAST, TRAVEL

The Six of Swords is a card indicating a recovery from difficult times. Hope is on the horizon, things will get better. It’s important to rest, revitalize, and surround yourself with joyful friends. Plan a trip, short or long. Even getting out of town for the weekend will further lift your spirits.

**Sit with:** What would you bring along on this crossing, and what can you leave on the shore?

#### Reversed

UNFINISHED RECOVERY, LINGERING STORM

The rainbow arches over the pile of swords, but you can't lift your head far enough to see it. The heavy rain hasn't finished, and the healing that began after the Five has stalled. Don't force yourself to just get over it. Something may still need telling, grieving, or forgiving before you can move on. Lie there a little longer, and be patient with how slowly the sky clears.

**Sit with:** What still needs telling, grieving, or forgiving before you can move on?

### Seven of Swords

#### Upright

SECRECY, SELF-INTEREST

Six swords hang in plain view, while the seventh is tucked under the sly fox’s tail. So goes the story of the Seven of Swords... a secret is being kept. You're either the keeper or the kept-from, and it's time to identify which. Deceit and avoidance linger in the air. Face the cause of secrecy or risk ostracizing yourself.

**Sit with:** What are you keeping under your tail, and who is it costing?

#### Reversed

EXPOSURE, CONSCIENCE

The fox curled on her sword loses her cover, and the seventh blade she was guarding is out in the open, the diagonal lines leading every eye to her. A secret may come to light, or you may be ready to stop keeping one. The reversed card carries the relief and discomfort of being found out, even by yourself. If you're the keeper, come clean before it costs more. If you've been kept from the truth, trust the small details that haven't added up.

**Sit with:** Which small details haven't added up, and what would it feel like to come clean?

### Eight of Swords

#### Upright

TRAPPED, POWERLESS

Surrounded by obstacles and threats on all sides, you find yourself the victim. You see no way out, no available choices. Your perceptions keep you from opening your wings and taking flight. What keeps you suspended here? Yourself or others? The Eight of Swords demands an answer. You cannot hang here much longer.

**Sit with:** What is truly binding you, and what is only a story you've come to believe?

#### Reversed

AWAKENING, SMALL STEPS

The butterfly bound tight to her sword begins to stir, and the seven swords leaning toward her turn out never to have touched her. The mental fog thins enough to find one small step, and the stable horizontal lines start to allow movement. Or you've told the story of being trapped for so long that it's become comfortable. Take the small step anyway. The wings open slowly, and no one else can open them for you.

**Sit with:** What is the smallest step you can take even though the wings haven't opened?

### Nine of Swords

#### Upright

DARK VISIONS, ANGUISH

An extremely dark card, the Nine of Swords reveals the deepest shadows of the self. It indicates insomnia, nightmares, and worries in the midnight hour. Often these states are accompanied by guilt, despair, or even depression. You'll be battling with yourself, so turn to others for help. Find joy. Reach out.

**Sit with:** Who could you tell about the midnight thoughts tonight?

#### Reversed

RELIEF, CLEARER THOUGHTS

The eyeballs and wriggling worms start to resolve into shapes you can look at, and you notice the nine swords have been orderly all along. The worst of the midnight hour has passed. Or the anguish has become so familiar that you've gone numb to it. Talk to someone, write it down, say it out loud. Most of what your mind pictured never came to be.

**Sit with:** What would happen if you said the worst of it out loud?

### Ten of Swords

#### Upright

RUIN, ROCK BOTTOM, MELODRAMA

When the Ten of Swords appears, you've finally hit rock bottom. You've let others walk all over you while you play the victim. Is it possible that you love the backstabbing and drama too much to move forward without it? If not for drama... what would you focus on? It's time to find out.

**Sit with:** If you weren't telling this story of being wronged, what would you focus on?

#### Reversed

GETTING UP, ENDING THE DRAMA

The bull, splayed out and merging with the dark, begins to stir. The drama burns down and you stop performing the suffering. This is the beginning of getting up, because you have already touched the bottom. Or you can't quite let go of the story that you were wronged. Ask what you'd have to feel if you stopped telling it. Then step forward to see.

**Sit with:** What would you have to feel if you stopped telling it?

### Daughter of Swords

#### Upright

HONEST, INSIGHTFUL

The Daughter of Swords is a young woman whose honesty and insights take her far in life. People truly value her frankness. She learns from keen observation... it almost seems as though she never stops "watching." Sometimes this becomes a burden for her, as she can’t help noticing this or that small detail that could’ve been done better. There's a potential for her to hold on to those experiences and become spiteful and judgmental.

**Sit with:** What have you noticed lately that you haven't said, and how could you say it gently?

#### Reversed

BLUNTNESS, SILENT JUDGMENT

The owlet on her straight black sword says exactly what she means, and without tenderness it lands like a blow. Or she watches and notices everything and says nothing, and the silence hardens into judgment. The rainbow stars behind her are a reminder that her view holds more colors than the plain one she insists on. If she's you, tell the truth with kindness. If she's someone in your life, listen for what's unsaid.

**Sit with:** Is what you're holding back a kindness, or a verdict?

### Son of Swords

#### Upright

FORCEFUL, DETERMINED

A dynamic creature, the Son of Swords is a man of action, not of grace. He pushes forward toward his goal with urgency and determination. To top it off, he's very well educated, making him a force to be reckoned with. Usually he’s seeking approval from the patient and just Father of Swords, who casts quite a shadow onto his son. Working with this stimulating and exhausting young man can prove to be a challenge.

**Sit with:** What are you pushing toward, and whose approval is hiding inside it?

#### Reversed

RECKLESS, ARGUMENTATIVE

The young owl is mid-flight, but the slanted lines no longer meet at a point worth aiming for. His conviction loses its aim, and he picks fights to feel certain. Or the confidence collapses and the sword hangs limp in his talons. If he's you, ask whether you're after the truth or only the win. If he's someone in your life, don't hand over your attention as ammunition.

**Sit with:** Are you after the truth, or only the win?

### Mother of Swords

#### Upright

SHARP, PERCEPTIVE

You'll often find the Mother of Swords putting her sharp perceptions to use as a therapist, doctor, or teacher. She's extremely experienced and knowledgeable in her field. Some think of her as all-seeing. This card can sometimes represent a woman going through a difficult time. She may be recently widowed or divorced, and usually without children. During this time, there’s potential for her criticism to soar.

**Sit with:** What have you seen clearly that cost you something to see?

#### Reversed

COLD, CRITICAL

The snowy owl's calm perch on her sword turns to aloofness, and her over-the-shoulder look turns to a glance that dismisses. The wit sharpens into sarcasm and the discernment into judgment. She may be using humor as armor and cutting before she can be cut. If she's you, ask what the sharpness protects. If she's someone in your life, remember she sees clearly, though not always kindly.

**Sit with:** What is your sharpness protecting?

### Father of Swords

#### Upright

FAIR, ANALYTICAL

Like all the Swords family, the Father has a gift for perception. He has a distinct ability to remove himself emotionally from a situation, so that he can truly see it from all angles. He is therefore respected by others, who usually describe him as fair or just. He's a responsible man with deep ties to his family.

**Sit with:** Where can you stand back and see every angle and still stay in the room?

#### Reversed

RIGID, JUDGMENTAL

The rainbow in his sword narrows to a single color. His head-on gaze, usually piercing, turns to a verdict. Fairness hardens into rules, and analysis stalls into never deciding. He can see every angle and still miss the person standing in front of him. If he's you, let one decision be made with your heart in the room. If he's someone in your life, don't let his verdict define your truth.

**Sit with:** What decision could you make with your heart present?

## Pentacles

The suit of worldly possessions, the Pentacles often speak to issues involving our home, money, or career. They reflect upon our generosity and greed. Pentacles are ruled by the earth element. When many appear in a reading, it means the focus is on material things—property, income, or the home.

### Ace of Pentacles

#### Upright

PROSPEROUS BEGINNINGS

In the center of even the giant redwood trees... a tiny seedling once stood. Such is the energy of the Ace of Pentacles—it's the seed that takes root, grounding you for the future. You're in the beginning phases of a prosperous venture. Stay grounded. Go outside and take time to appreciate nature. You may also find a windfall of wealth headed your way.

**Sit with:** What seed has landed in your life that you could plant with care?

#### Reversed

MISSED OPPORTUNITY, DELAYED GROWTH

The tree stump's rings and glowing pentacle at the center are all there, and the branches that should grow in several directions stay bare. A prosperous beginning is delayed, or you've overlooked the patient groundwork it needs. The seed isn't lost. It may need patience, a plan, or attention to the soil. Return to your center, since that's where all the growth begins. Check your resources honestly, then plant one real, small thing.

**Sit with:** Which small, real thing could you plant today?

### Two of Pentacles

#### Upright

BALANCE, CHANGE

The Two of Pentacles signifies inevitable change. Since the Pentacles suit relates to earthly possessions, this usually means a new job or financial situation or a move. Even if you fear this change, it needs to happen and might even be fun. Face it with the grace of a newly formed butterfly... a world of possibilities balanced upon your delicate wings.

**Sit with:** What change are you afraid of that might actually be fun?

#### Reversed

OVERWHELM, DROPPED BALLS

The butterfly's wings are open and the two pentacles still trace their rainbow lemniscate, but the slanted lines no longer meet in a calm middle. You're juggling more than you can keep up in the air, or you've stopped adjusting, certain that last month's balance will hold. Something has to be set down. Decide what you can drop without breaking anything, and let a little play back into it.

**Sit with:** What can you set down without breaking anything?

### Three of Pentacles

#### Upright

TEAMWORK, DETERMINATION, FOCUS

The task ahead is a momentous one. Usually related to your job or career, the Three of Pentacles suggests you must focus all of your efforts. Discipline, strategy, and hard work are needed more than ever. If you become weary or overwhelmed, rely on those around you. This is a card of teamwork, so you may need the strength of others to conquer the mountain.

**Sit with:** Who has the skills you need, and have you asked?

#### Reversed

POOR TEAMWORK, LACK OF PLANNING

The red, blue, and yellow pentacles beneath the three-peaked mountain stop lending each other their color, and the lines between them don't make new hues. A team falls out of step, or you refuse help because taking it feels like weakness. Or the blueprint is half-built and no one shares the vision. The mountain won't be climbed by one set of hands. Ask for what you need, and honor the skills others bring.

**Sit with:** What are you refusing help with because accepting it feels like weakness?

### Four of Pentacles

#### Upright

POSSESSION, CONTROL

The Four of Pentacles first suggests material gain and stability. Only good news for your bank account and home. Yet underneath the surface this is a card of warning: do not become possessive or controlling. Holding on too tightly to the material world will leave you rigid, stagnant, and displeasing to others. Wealth is a concept.

**Sit with:** What are you holding so tightly that it can't grow?

#### Reversed

TIGHTENING GRIP, RELEASING CONTROL

The blue, magenta, and yellow threads binding the diamond of pentacles either squeeze tighter, so that holding becomes hoarding and safety becomes a cage, or they begin to loosen, and a little control goes. The background lines still can't penetrate. Either way, ask what you're keeping safe and from what. Not everything needs managing. Some of it can be trusted to the world.

**Sit with:** What are you keeping safe, and from what?

### Five of Pentacles

#### Upright

SADNESS, WORRY, ILLNESS

The Five of Pentacles is a card of hard times. It may come in the form of an illness, job loss, financial trouble, or rejection. Above all this there will be worry, so much worry. This anxiety is counterproductive and damaging. You must find a way to quiet the mind during this difficult time. Rely on meditation or visualization to find peace.

**Sit with:** Who could you tell what you need, and what makes it hard to ask?

#### Reversed

RECOVERY, ASKING FOR HELP

The drooping rose straightens, and the five pentacles in their half circle of light at the top of the card come into reach. A hard stretch of illness, worry, or scarcity begins to ease, or you finally ask for help. Or shame keeps you silent, convinced the warmth is for everyone but you. The storm lines remain, but they are no longer the whole sky. Say what you need to someone who can hear it.

**Sit with:** What shame is keeping you from the warmth that's within reach?

### Six of Pentacles

#### Upright

PROSPERITY, GROWTH, GENEROSITY

The Six of Pentacles indicates that your long-awaited fruits are ready for harvesting, bringing more wealth than anticipated. Make sure you are generous during this bountiful time. This card can also signify generosity coming from someone else. If this is the case, accept the help with grace and put the resources to good use.

**Sit with:** What have you been given, and what could you give in return?

#### Reversed

UNEVEN EXCHANGE, STRINGS ATTACHED

The white branch that flourished with its six ripe pentacles grows unevenly, heavy on one side. Giving comes with strings, or taking happens without thanks. You may be hoarding the harvest, or giving until nothing is left. Notice where the exchange lost its reciprocity. Generosity that costs you your dignity isn't generosity, and neither is help that comes with a debt.

**Sit with:** Where did this exchange lose its reciprocity?

### Seven of Pentacles

#### Upright

PERIOD OF CONTEMPLATION

A curious card, the Seven of Pentacles shows a period of contemplation and uncertainty. You're looking back at all of your hard work and wondering if it has been a failure or a success. You can’t seem to decide. Shouldn’t you be reaping more rewards by now? Maybe so. But the mind has control over perception. It's possible your rewards are waiting for you to recognize them. They are not always monetary.

**Sit with:** What reward have you overlooked because it didn't look the way you expected?

#### Reversed

IMPATIENCE, MISJUDGED PROGRESS

The seven dark pentacles, lined up as though against a wall, scatter or get pulled up. You want results sooner than they can ripen, or you've stopped checking the garden altogether. You may be uprooting things to see whether they've grown. Step back and take real stock of what is working and what isn't. Some of your harvest is already here, waiting to be noticed.

**Sit with:** What are you uprooting too soon just to check whether it has grown?

### Eight of Pentacles

#### Upright

CRAFTSMANSHIP, SKILL

The spider is a true master of her craft. She weaves against all odds with skill, intricacy, and confidence. The Eight of Pentacles requires a similar approach. Hone your skills. You are close to finding mastery at your craft, so strive for it. Pay attention to all the details. In some cases this card means you need to find a new hobby, something you truly enjoy. Start a project. Become good at it.

**Sit with:** What craft are you drawn to practice for its own sake?

#### Reversed

BURNOUT, PERFECTIONISM

The spider's intricate web, with four pentacles on either side, starts to fray. You're going through the motions, or fussing over details until nothing is finished. Perfectionism and burnout can look alike here. Ask whether you still love this work. If you do, set one thing down so you can do the rest well. If you don't, that's worth knowing too.

**Sit with:** Do you still love this work, and what could you set down to do the rest well?

### Nine of Pentacles

#### Upright

HAPPY, HEALTHY HOME

Always a welcome card, the Nine of Pentacles is a time to enjoy the many results of your loyalty and hard work. This may be a promotion at work or newfound stability and happiness at home. It’s even possible that you'll find yourself enjoying the finer things in life, as luxury and refinement oftentimes accompany this card.

**Sit with:** What does it feel like to be at home in what you've built?

#### Reversed

HOLLOW COMFORT, LOST SOVEREIGNTY

The four feathers still overlap and the nine pentacles still gleam, but the warm red-to-yellow lines around them have faded. You may be surrounded by what you worked for and feel no sovereignty over it. Comfort can dull, and luxury can become a way of hiding. Ask what this nest is for. If it doesn't feel like home yet, you may need to build inner resources, not outer ones.

**Sit with:** What would make this nest feel like home?

### Ten of Pentacles

#### Upright

FULFILLMENT, ABUNDANCE

The Ten of Pentacles signifies material and spiritual abundance in nearly every area of your life. The number ten usually indicates completion, and in this case the journey was well worth it. So be generous, not only with your money, but also with your wisdom. Provide guidance to those who struggle. You will be rewarded tenfold.

**Sit with:** What wisdom could you hand down that no one has asked for yet?

#### Reversed

STRAINED FOUNDATIONS, INHERITED BURDENS

The circular rainbow that radiates from the pentacle within the center pentacle dims, and the diamond loses its open, generous spread. What was handed down chafes, or the stability you were promised is slow to arrive. Look at what you've inherited and keep what still serves. A foundation can be rebuilt without being torn down.

**Sit with:** Which of what you inherited still serves you, and what can you put down?

### Daughter of Pentacles

#### Upright

RESPONSIBLE, KIND, SUPPORTIVE

You'll usually find the Daughter of Pentacles behind the scenes rather than out in the spotlight. Though quite shy, she's extremely hardworking and responsible. She handles details and logistics well—people depend on her. She has vast amounts of inner strength. Like all of the Pentacles family, the Daughter thrives when in nature.

**Sit with:** What quiet work do you do that deserves to be thanked?

#### Reversed

INSECURITY, FEAR OF MISTAKES

She stands on the hilltop under her rainbow, and her legs buckle. She's afraid of mistakes, so she doesn't try. Or she rushes ahead without learning from the first attempts. Trial and error is her gift, but it needs patience with the errors. If she's you, take one small, low-stakes step. If she's someone in your life, encourage the trying.

**Sit with:** What one low-stakes step could you take today?

### Son of Pentacles

#### Upright

LOYAL, QUIET, DEDICATED

The good side of the Son of Pentacles is that he’s loyal and determined. He's inventive and can be trusted wholeheartedly. But sometimes this figure becomes stubborn and persistent to a fault. This, combined with his quiet nature, results in his being hard to get to know and socially awkward at times. He commonly has only one or two close friends rather than many.

**Sit with:** What are you loyal to, and does it still deserve your loyalty?

#### Reversed

STUBBORNNESS, STAGNATION

He leans into the card pressing forward, eyes on the path, and the path has become a groove. His steady plodding turns to stubbornness, or he stalls and calls it caution. He won't look up long enough to see that the orange crescent moon and the pentacle above him mark a different route. If he's you, lift your head and check the weather. If he's someone in your life, don't expect change to come easily.

**Sit with:** What have you stopped looking up long enough to see?

### Mother of Pentacles

#### Upright

DOMESTIC, LOVING, PATIENT

The Mother of Pentacles excels in the home. She knows exactly what to do while raising a family and tending to the tasks of daily life. There's a potential for her identity to become wrapped entirely around her children and home, which leaves her needy and attached. This is the cause of most of her turmoil. She often has qualities of a healer and is very connected to nature.

**Sit with:** Where do you tend the people and places you love, and how do they tend you?

#### Reversed

OVER-GIVING, DEPLETION

The fawn sleeps soundly against her, but the mother can no longer rest. She serves until she's empty, and her capability turns to martyrdom. The greens and blues of earthly receptivity may go gray, or she neglects the very home and body she's meant to tend. Resting doesn't make you less useful. If she's you, ask who tends to the one who tends everyone else. If she's someone in your life, offer care before she has to ask.

**Sit with:** Who tends to the one who tends everyone?

### Father of Pentacles

#### Upright

STEADY, ENTREPRENEURIAL

The Father of Pentacles is a steady, gentle man. Upon first meeting, he can almost seem dull because of his extremely calm temperament. But underneath is an incredibly passionate man who prioritizes the stability of his job, family, and home. He is entrepreneurial and diligent at work. A true pleasure to know.

**Sit with:** What does steady look like when you aren't trying to prove anything?

#### Reversed

WORKAHOLISM, WORTH TIED TO WORK

The stag's rainbow antlers grow heavy. His pride in competence turns into a belief that he's only as good as his results. The black lines that pulse out from the pentacle between his antlers push toward fixing, doing, and providing, and he forgets to be present. His self-worth is on the line with every outcome. If he's you, find one part of yourself that isn't for sale. If he's someone in your life, offer him rest without conditions.

**Sit with:** What part of you isn't for sale?

## The Major Arcana

### The Fool

#### Upright

SPONTANEITY, INNOCENCE

The Fool is ready to fly. His young wings ache for flight, and with a single step he leaves behind the comfort of the nest to begin the journey through the Major Arcana. Is he ready? Will he fall? This is a card about beginnings. It points to the side of you that is spontaneous, excited, naive, and inexperienced. Others may doubt your abilities. Be ready to be called the "fool." Be ready to fall. No matter what people say about you, this is your journey—and it’s already begun.

**Sit with:** What step are you about to take that others may call foolish?

#### Reversed

HESITATION, RECKLESSNESS

The chick on the blossoming branch draws back the foot he'd stretched into the black void. The dawn is still orange and yellow behind him, and the horizontal lines still hold their free-flowing, undirected energy, but something says *not yet, not until you're sure*. Or the reverse: he steps off without looking at all, mistaking the wide sky for a guarantee. Ask whether you're preparing or stalling, or leaping with no thought of where you land. The unknown never offers certainty. It only asks for a small, honest step.

**Sit with:** Are you preparing, stalling, or leaping without looking?

### The Magician

#### Upright

SELF-EMPOWERMENT, ACTION

The Magician is a card of boundless, expansive energy. Whereas many of the Major Arcana deal with stillness or aspects of the mind... this card is all about action, action, action. It's time to see yourself as the wildcat—embrace his speed, grace, and abilities. Don't be afraid to begin. You have the power of all four elements within your reach. Now is the time to use them.

**Sit with:** Which tool within reach will you pick up first?

#### Reversed

SCATTERED POWER, MANIPULATION

The leopard's gaze drifts from the sword, cup, wand, and pentacle laid before him, and the rays no longer gather to a point. You have everything you need and attention spread too thin to use any of it. Or the confident leopard turns showy, using the infinity sign on his chest to impress instead of create. Come back to one tool, just as the leopard rests his paws on the blade. The light only has a direction when you give it one.

**Sit with:** Which single tool could you return to?

### The High Priestess

#### Upright

MYSTERY, PSYCHIC WISDOM

The High Priestess sits in stillness. From there she's able to access realms others pass by without noticing, the vast world of intuition, dreams, and mystery. She uses silence and non-action to harness this power. Sometimes this card appears to remind you to listen more closely to the voice within. Other times it's a sign to look past the obvious, to find what's being kept secret or hidden within a given situation. Acknowledge the shadows.

**Sit with:** What does stillness want to tell you?

#### Reversed

IGNORED INTUITION, HIDDEN SECRETS

The tiger's paws, spread easily around the blue and purple sphere, close over it. You hear your inner knowing, but you grip it, analyze it, or drown it in noise. The crescent moon is still in her black sky, but she faces away from the mystery she means to sit with. Or you keep a secret from yourself because the answer is inconvenient. Return to stillness, even for a few minutes. The sphere only glows when it isn't being held.

**Sit with:** What are you gripping that would show you more if you let go?

### The Empress

#### Upright

CREATION, NATURE, THE MOTHER

The Empress is the mother, or the goddess, of the tarot. Her energy encompasses all that is warm, fertile, creative, and sensual in the world. Her strength comes from being gentle and compassionate, and loving without binding. When this card appears, it's pointing to either a maternal relationship in your life or the side of yourself that wants to love more. The Empress also suggests it’s time for you to reconnect with nature. Go outside tonight and find the moon.

**Sit with:** Where does the love in you want to go outside tonight?

#### Reversed

DEPLETION, SMOTHERING

The lush deciduous tree under her waning crescent has dropped her blossoms, with the fruit a season away. You may be depleted from giving, or neglecting your body, or smothering what you love in the name of care. The pink, red, and purple at her edges mute. She teaches that a tree must rest to bear again. Tend yourself with the reverence you give to others, and let her cycle turn.

**Sit with:** What would you do if you let yourself rest before you bear again?

### The Emperor

#### Upright

PROTECTION, STABILITY, THE FATHER

The Emperor is the father. He's the tarot’s perfect counterpart to the Empress. He represents the side of you that's protective, decisive, and truly stable. He brings clarity of mind, and this in turn leads to actions or decisions. Sometimes this card is literal and points to a paternal relationship in your life. At other times, it suggests reconnecting with the part of you that stands strong and tall and knows just what to do. With help from the sun, the Emperor can see for miles and miles.

**Sit with:** Where do you already know what to do, and what is keeping you from doing it?

#### Reversed

RIGIDITY, CONTROL

The evergreen under his black sun holds so rigidly that he can't bend when the wind comes. Order becomes control, and discipline becomes punishment. Or the structure you needed was never built, and everything drifts in a sky with no sun to steer by. Ask whether your rules serve your life or only protect your fear. A good boundary frees you. A bad one is another cage.

**Sit with:** Do your rules serve your life, or only your fear?

### The Hierophant

#### Upright

MENTOR, SEEKING KNOWLEDGE

In its simplest form, the Hierophant represents a mentor or teacher. This card signifies a hunger for knowledge, whether it is in the practical or the spiritual realm. You've come as far as you can on your own, now it's the time to deepen your practice. Don't hesitate—join a class, go to a workshop, and don't be afraid of new experiences. A new ceremony or ritual may prove to be both comforting and rewarding. Open your heart, and your teacher will soon appear.

**Sit with:** What do you most hunger to learn, and who might teach you?

#### Reversed

OUTGROWN BELIEFS, REBELLION

The crow with the ruffled wings is shouting, but the jagged stream of color that charged the key no longer reaches it. His answers feel hollow, or you break from the tradition so fast that you never learn what it offered. The thick vertical lines still hold their weight, but you have your own key. Notice which beliefs you inherited and which you chose. Let the crow teach, but test what he says.

**Sit with:** Which beliefs did you inherit, and which did you choose?

### The Lovers

#### Upright

UNION, DESIRE, JOY

Two Canadian geese are mates for life, traveling partners within an expansive sky. The Lovers card is a welcome sight in any reading, as it suggests a beautiful and strong relationship on the horizon. Yes, you'll experience all the thrills of desire and romance, but meanwhile you'll be building a solid foundation together. The Lovers honor and respect each other, and with that they can go anywhere. If you're already in a relationship, it’s time to be grateful and nourish this rare and precious gem.

**Sit with:** What do you need to say to honor the one beside you?

#### Reversed

DISHARMONY, MISALIGNED VALUES

The two geese facing forward on parallel paths fall out of rhythm. One flaps hard while the other glides, and the rainbow background radiates in all directions except toward each other. Values drift apart, or you lose yourself in the pairing, or you're unsure you're in the right flock. Harmony takes honesty. Ask what each of you needs to fly your own path, then see where those paths still run together.

**Sit with:** Where do your paths still run together?

### The Chariot

#### Upright

STRONG WILL, TRIUMPH

The Chariot is your confidence, your will, and your inner warrior. At points in your life when you felt the bliss of achievement or triumph, you were riding on his back. Build a relationship with this part of yourself. Try to "see" the chariot inside you. The more focused your mind, the easier it will be to sense his presence and stay motivated on his back. With a fixed gaze and sure footing, you'll be headed toward all you dream of.

**Sit with:** Where do you feel your will running steady, and what is it carrying you toward?

#### Reversed

LOSS OF CONTROL, AGGRESSION

The horse facing us head-on, mane flying, either bolts with the bit between her teeth or digs in and won't go. Willpower tips into force, or the confidence that carried you goes slack. The black crescent moon on her forehead is still asking you to listen to the part of you that knows. Ask who is steering. If it's the ego alone, slow down. If it's fear, take one step anyway.

**Sit with:** Who is steering right now, your ego or your fear?

### Justice

#### Upright

DECISIONS, KARMA

With tails entwined, two cats look directly at you... waiting for you to choose between them. Which is right and which is wrong? The Justice card implies a weight or heaviness surrounding a choice you have to make. Now is not the time to shun the concept of divine balance, or karma. All of your choices affect your life, and sometimes the lives of those around you, both now and in the future.

**Sit with:** Which choice are you weighing, and who else does it touch?

#### Reversed

AVOIDED ACCOUNTABILITY, UNFAIRNESS

The two cats turn from each other, their entwined tails come apart, and the sword hangs above a diamond that no longer holds both halves. You're avoiding accountability, or seeing only black and white where the gray area is. Something unfair may be going unaddressed. Look honestly at your part. Justice delayed isn't justice denied, but it asks you to act with integrity today.

**Sit with:** What is your part in this that you haven't looked at yet?

### The Hermit

#### Upright

SOLITUDE, MEDITATION

The idea of being a "hermit" is not supported in our society, but in the realm of tarot hermits are highly celebrated and valued creatures. Through meditation, solitude, and stillness they bring wisdom to all. When this card appears, it's time to step back from the business of day-to-day life and focus on your inner realm. Become more self-aware. If you're intrigued by meditation, start now. Spend time by yourself. Your inner fire is ready to be lit... it will shine for all to see.

**Sit with:** What is your inner flame asking for when you step back from the noise?

#### Reversed

ISOLATION, AVOIDANCE

The tortoise either stays inside long after his lantern has burned low, or comes out before he's finished listening. Solitude has turned into hiding, or noise has drowned out the white patch of light on the ground that shows the next step. Ask whether you're withdrawing to find yourself or to avoid something. Either way, the flame is on your back. Choose where to look.

**Sit with:** Are you withdrawing to find yourself, or to avoid something?

### Wheel of Fortune

#### Upright

DESTINY, CHANGE OF COURSE

Whether day or night, the Wheel of Fortune is always in motion. Some call it fate; others, destiny. It's possible to go for months or years without feeling its presence... but when this card appears, the eye of the wheel is fixed upon you. Don't be surprised if there's a change of course heading your way, something you'd least expect. Whatever happens to you during this time, know that it is bringing you closer to your life’s purpose.

**Sit with:** What would you do if you trusted the turning?

#### Reversed

RESISTING CHANGE, DELAY

The owl at the top of the wheel watches calmly, but you're clutching the tangled branches. A change that wants to arrive is being delayed, or all you can see is the tangle and none of the rainbow threads. The wheel will turn either way. Ask what you can influence and release what you can't. Move with the turning, as the owl does.

**Sit with:** What can you influence here, and what can you release?

### Strength

#### Upright

MASTERY OF EMOTIONS

It’s common to think of this card as the roaring, devouring side of the lion. But look again—the "strength" this card suggests is a much deeper force that’s found within. The lion represents our most patient, composed self. He’s a master of focus, compassion, and self-control. When this card comes up, you're in need of harnessing this power for yourself. All the courage you need can be found in the muscle known as the heart.

**Sit with:** Where is your gentleness the strongest thing in the room?

#### Reversed

SELF-DOUBT, LOST PATIENCE

The lion's gentle hold on the rose slips. Your courage wavers, or your patience snaps and the teeth show. You may be hiding your gentleness because it feels weak, or acting hard to cover a fear. The lemniscate on the lion's forehead is a reminder that the power is limitless and doesn't need to be loud. Return to your breath, and hold the rose without crushing it.

**Sit with:** How could you hold the rose without crushing it?

### The Hanged Man

#### Upright

SACRIFICE, LETTING GO

Many people talk about the act of "letting go." But what does it really mean? How do you achieve it? The Hanged Man has all the answers; he’s the master of non-attachment. This card implies there’s a sacrifice or a difficult or painful situation coming up in your life. Though you naturally want to resist and struggle through it, be more like The Hanged Man. Find stillness, open your eyes, and use this new perspective to learn something. You're stuck here either way.

**Sit with:** What can you learn from this stillness that you couldn't learn moving?

#### Reversed

STALLING, PREMATURE SURRENDER

The bat clings to his perch for no reason, or drops before his wings can catch him. You're delaying a surrender you know is needed, or jumping before the time is right. The red glow of his eyes holds a secret you haven't asked for. Ask what you're holding onto and what it's costing you. Flight comes when you let go at the right moment.

**Sit with:** What are you holding onto, and what is it costing you?

### Death

#### Upright

CLOSURE, TRANSFORMATION

No one loves the sight of the Death card in a reading. We spend most of our lives denying death altogether... so when this card appears, it's easy to get frightened or think it’s "bad." Don't let the drama distract you from the message: something in your life needs to end. It needs closure. This will happen voluntarily or involuntarily. Either way, you will feel a positive transformation begin after the initial forms of suffering have passed.

**Sit with:** What has already ended that you've been pretending hasn't?

#### Reversed

CLINGING, LINGERING ENDINGS

The bird is long since gone, and still something won't be laid to rest. You cling to what has decayed, or the ending came so quietly you didn't notice you survived it. The black and white card is impersonal, and so is this. Honor what ended, and let the spring begin without you having to push it.

**Sit with:** What needs to be honored before the spring can begin?

### Temperance

#### Upright

HEALING, RENEWAL

The great blue heron remains calm and peaceful as she blends the opposing elements of fire and water. The Temperance card asks you to be a moderator much like the heron. Focus on cooperation and compromise. If you've been excessive in one aspect of your life, practice self-restraint and moderation now. You'll find a new sense of healing and balance from bringing a little harmony back into your life.

**Sit with:** Where are you overdoing, and what small, measured step would restore balance?

#### Reversed

IMBALANCE, EXCESS

The heron's outstretched wing droops, and the blue droplets either drown the orange flame or the fire scorches the water. Extremes take over, and the blend you've been tending slips from your control. The inward-slanting lines lose their center. Ask what you've been overdoing and what you've been neglecting, then return to small, measured steps. Balance is an art, and you can begin again.

**Sit with:** What have you been neglecting while you overdid the rest?

### The Devil

#### Upright

ADDICTION, NEGATIVITY

Though it may feel frightening when the Devil appears in your reading, this card carries an urgent message and must be interpreted with total honesty. The darkness of the Devil card takes the form of addiction, negativity, or materialism. It can indicate an ugly relationship with a person or substance. Identify this dependency and how it's holding you back. The devil's hooves are strong and relentless—their grip will not loosen unless a conscious change is made. Free yourself.

**Sit with:** What chain have you been wearing that you could name out loud?

#### Reversed

LOOSENING GRIP, BREAKING FREE

The goat's smirk no longer holds you. In the dark, bleak card you see the chain for what it is, and for the first time you wonder if you could simply step out. The flames at his feet never burned him, and they needn't burn you. Release is rarely a single moment. It's the quiet decision to name the dependency out loud and take one small action to free yourself.

**Sit with:** What is one small action that moves you toward freedom?

### The Tower

#### Upright

UNEXPECTED UPHEAVAL

When the Tower card appears, it’s time to brace yourself for a change. The well-rooted tree that's been growing strong for decades is crashing down around you. Your world may feel as though it’s literally falling apart—and you didn't see it coming. Even though this phase is painful and confusing, it will be over soon. You'll look back and feel grateful things changed course. You might even see it as a personal "breakthrough" in the end.

**Sit with:** What fell that you were secretly tired of propping up?

#### Reversed

DELAYED UPHEAVAL, RELEASE

The evergreen with its top already torn off by lightning either has come down, or you've spent so long bracing for the second bolt that you're worn out. This card asks what you're still holding up. Some structures in our lives are already rubble, and we keep propping up the walls out of habit or pride. Other times the collapse is slow, roots loosening over years instead of in one storm. Let the dust settle before you rebuild, and notice how much more sky there is.

**Sit with:** What are you still holding up out of habit or pride?

### The Star

#### Upright

HOPE, PEACE OF MIND

Standing outside on a dark night, you gaze up at the stars and sense glimmers of wonder and hope. Even though you can't find concrete answers to life's many questions up there in the sky, you can't help but feel comforted and renewed. Such is the energy of the Star card. It is not about actions or situations. It's simply about connecting to the part of you that is hopeful and serene. This force will bring light back into your life and infuse you with positivity. A lovely card.

#### Reversed

LOST HOPE, DISCONNECTION

### The Moon

VIVID DREAMS AND FEARS

In many ways the Moon card encompasses the idea of The Wild Unknown. It is the shadow realm, the place where dreams, fears, and mysteries are born. Much darkness can linger here, and if you aren't careful, this can lead to periods of anxiety and self-doubt... almost as though you've lost your way in a house of mirrors. Many great artists have roamed this inner landscape. It's where imagination and creativity drift freely upon the midnight air.

**Sit with:** What are your fears trying to show you about what you want?

#### Reversed

CLEARING FOG, AVOIDED FEARS

The pale moon between the two sparse evergreens steadies, and for the first time the ground comes into view. The anxiety that made everything feel unreal begins to thin. **Sit with:** What remains when the fog clears?

### The Sun

VITALITY, ENLIGHTENMENT

Imagine for a moment that you're soaking in the warm rays of the sun. It feels nourishing and healing; all your aches and pains just fade away. The Sun card brings this amazing energy into your life. Vitality and health abound, while you feel assurance and clarity in all you do. Spend some extra time outside today, be grateful for the radiance of the sun and the life force it gives all creatures.

**Sit with:** Where can you let warmth reach you today?

#### Reversed

DIMMED VITALITY, DELAYED JOY

**Sit with:** Where do you need shade, and where can you let the warmth in?

### Judgement

#### Upright

FORGIVENESS

The word "judgement" conjures up fear and guilt in many people. This card, however, concerns itself with another aspect of this word—seeking the truth. No more blaming yourself or others, and no more excuses. Now is the time for forgiveness and personal freedom. This card asks you to rise up and let pettiness and fear fall below you. Expand your wings and be reborn. What a relief it will be.

#### Reversed

SELF-JUDGMENT, IGNORING THE CALL

The white bird spreads her rayed wings, and the bats stay in the dark. You hear the call and talk yourself out of it, or you can't forgive yourself enough to rise. The past is held more tightly than it holds you. Ask what you would have to believe to answer. Then answer, even quietly.

**Sit with:** What would you have to believe to answer the call?

### The World

#### Upright

WHOLENESS, COMPLETION

The World is the final card in the journey of the Major Arcana. It signifies completion, harmony, and contentment. It's rare to experience this energy, as we are usually consumed by wanting, needing, and achieving. So when this card appears, contemplate for a moment the idea of “being whole." Focus on the image on the card... close your eyes and envision this radiating energy inside you. What does it feel like? Where is it located? What blocks you from feeling a sense of “wholeness” each and every day of your life?

**Sit with:** Where do you feel whole, and what keeps you from feeling it every day?

#### Reversed

INCOMPLETION, LOOSE ENDS

The sphere of interlocking rings is nearly whole, and one ring won't close. A loose end, a thing left unsaid, or a feeling you can't step back far enough to see. The white core still shines, and the starry sky is still lightening toward the bottom of the card. The ending is delayed, not denied. Ask what needs integrating before you can call this complete. You are already whole, even if the story isn't finished.

**Sit with:** What needs integrating before you can call this complete?
